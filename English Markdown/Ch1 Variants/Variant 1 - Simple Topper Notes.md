# CBSE Class 11 English Hornbill Chapter 1 Notes - Variant 1
## The Portrait of a Lady (Prose) + A Photograph (Poem)

# PART A: PROSE - THE PORTRAIT OF A LADY by Khushwant Singh

### 1. About the Author (2-3 Lines)
Khushwant Singh (1915-2014) was a famous Indian writer, novelist and journalist. He wrote in simple and honest English. This true story is taken from his own childhood and is a loving tribute to his grandmother.

### 2. Theme / Central Idea
The story shows selfless love, old values, and the pain of growing apart. It shows the beautiful relationship between a grandson and his grandmother. It also shows the clash between traditional village life and modern city life. Main message: true love needs no words, and a noble life ends in peace.

### 3. Characters
**1. The Grandmother:** Old, short, fat and slightly bent. White silver hair. Always in white clothes. Very religious, kind, loving and strong. Loved animals and prayer.
**2. The Narrator (Khushwant Singh himself):** A young boy who grows into a young man. Loves his grandmother but slowly becomes busy with modern studies in city and abroad.

### 4. Perfect 150-Word Summary Paragraph (For 6-Mark Question)
The Portrait of a Lady is Khushwant Singh's loving tribute to his grandmother. He remembers her as a short, fat, slightly bent old lady with silver hair and a serene, saintly face. In the village, they were good friends. She woke him up, got him ready, walked him to the temple school, helped with lessons, and fed stray dogs. After they moved to the city, a distance grew. He went to an English school by bus, learnt science and music, which she disliked as she linked music to beggars. She accepted loneliness with her spinning wheel and prayers. In the city, she fed sparrows daily. When he went abroad for five years, she saw him off calmly. On his return, she celebrated by singing and beating a drum. Next day she fell ill, felt her end was near, prayed quietly, and died peacefully. Thousands of sparrows mourned silently around her body.

### 5. Chapter Synopsis in Simple English
**Stage 1 - Village Childhood:** The narrator lived with his grandmother in the village. She was his best friend. She woke him early, bathed him, gave him breakfast of stale chapatti with butter and sugar, and took him to the temple school. The priest taught the alphabet and morning prayer. On the way back, she fed village dogs with stale chapattis.

**Stage 2 - City Life:** They moved to the city. The narrator went to an English school in a motor bus. Grandmother could no longer go with him. She could not help in English lessons. She was sad there was no teaching about God in school. She disliked science and music lessons. She thought music was only for harlots and beggars, not for gentle people.

**Stage 3 - Seclusion and Sparrows:** Grandmother accepted her loneliness. Her room became her world. She sat spinning the wheel and reciting prayers from morning to evening. Her only joy was feeding hundreds of sparrows in the courtyard. The sparrows sat on her head and shoulders without fear.

**Stage 4 - Going Abroad and Death:** When the narrator went abroad for higher studies for five years, grandmother went to the railway station to see him off. She was not emotional. She kissed his forehead. On his return after five years, she was very happy. She sang old songs of homecoming warriors and beat an old drum for hours. Next morning she had slight fever. She said her end was near. She lay in bed, praying and telling her beads, and died peacefully. Thousands of sparrows came, sat silently around her dead body, and flew away without chirping when the body was taken for cremation.

### 6. Key Points to Remember for Exam
1. Grandmother was short, fat, slightly bent, with silver hair.
2. Her face was calm and saint-like.
3. In village: temple school, morning prayer, feeding dogs.
4. In city: English school, motor bus, no God teaching.
5. She disliked music and science.
6. Her only companions in city: spinning wheel and sparrows.
7. She went to station at narrator's departure and return.
8. She sang and beat drum on his return - did not pray that evening.
9. Next day she died telling beads.
10. Sparrows mourned silently - proof of her love for all living beings.

### 7. Difficult Word Meanings (12 Words)
1. **Portrait -** picture or description of a person
2. **Serene -** calm and peaceful
3. **Contentment -** state of being happy and satisfied
4. **Monotonous -** boring, same again and again
5. **Seclusion -** being alone, away from people
6. **Repose -** rest, peace
7. **Harlots -** women of bad character (as grandmother believed)
8. **Pallor -** pale yellow colour of old face
9. **Shroud -** white cloth to cover dead body
10. **Cremation -** burning of dead body as per Hindu custom
11. **Persuade -** to request and convince
12. **Overstraining -** putting too much pressure on body

### 8. Important Lines Explained
**1. "Her face was a criss-cross of wrinkles running from everywhere to everywhere."**
Means grandmother was very old. Her whole face had deep lines. It shows her age and life of hard work.

**2. "She was like the winter landscape in the mountains."**
Her white hair and white clothes looked pure like snow on mountains. She was calm, pure and beautiful like nature.

**3. "Music had lewd associations."**
Grandmother was old-fashioned. She thought music was only for beggars and bad women. So she was shocked when her grandson learnt music in city school.

**4. "When we carried her corpse off, they flew away quietly."**
When grandmother died, thousands of sparrows sat silently and did not eat crumbs. When her body was taken away, they flew silently. It shows even birds loved and respected her.

### 9. Five Likely Exam Q&A

**Q1. (Short) How did the grandmother look?**
**A:** She was short, fat and slightly bent. She had silver white hair. She wore white clothes. She had deep wrinkles. She always counted beads of her rosary. Her face looked calm and saintly.

**Q2. (Short) Why did grandmother dislike the city school?**
**A:** In city school there was no teaching about God. English words, science and music were taught. She did not understand them. She thought music was for beggars only. So she disliked the school.

**Q3. (Short) What was grandmother's daily routine in the village?**
**A:** She woke the narrator, bathed him, gave him chapatti with butter and sugar, took him to temple school, helped him in lessons, and fed stray dogs on the way back with stale chapattis.

**Q4. (Long) Describe the grandmother's life in the city. How did she spend her time?**
**A:** In the city, grandmother became lonely. She could not go to school with the boy. She could not help in his studies. So she lived alone in her room. She spent the day spinning her wheel and praying. Her only happiness was feeding sparrows in the afternoon. Hundreds of sparrows came and sat on her. She smiled but never talked much. She accepted this loneliness calmly.

**Q5. (Long) How did the grandmother die? What did the sparrows do?**
**A:** On the narrator's return from abroad, grandmother sang songs and beat a drum. She was tired. Next morning she was ill with slight fever. She said her end was near. She refused to talk to family. She lay in bed praying and telling beads till she died. After death, thousands of sparrows gathered silently in the courtyard. They did not chirp or eat crumbs. When her body was taken for cremation, they flew away quietly. This silent mourning shows her deep bond with birds.

# PART B: POEM - A PHOTOGRAPH by Shirley Toulson

### 1. About the Poet (2-3 Lines)
Shirley Toulson (1924-2018) was an English poet, writer and journalist. She wrote about love, memory and loss. This poem is about her mother's old photograph and the pain of losing her mother.

### 2. Theme / Central Idea
The theme is loss, memory, and the fast passage of time. Life changes and ends in death, but a photograph freezes one happy moment forever. The poem shows that death brings silence. Nature (sea) stays the same, but humans die.

### 3. Characters in the Poem
**1. The Poet's Mother:** As a 12-year-old girl, smiling and happy on beach holiday. Later as a grown woman who laughs at the photo. Later she dies.
**2. Betty and Dolly:** Mother's two cousins who went for sea holiday with her and held hands.
**3. The Uncle:** Who took the photograph.
**4. The Poet (Shirley Toulson):** Daughter who now looks at the photo and feels sad and speechless.

### 4. Perfect 150-Word Summary Paragraph (For 6-Mark Question)
A Photograph by Shirley Toulson is a tender elegy on loss and the painful passage of time. The poet looks at an old cardboard-framed photograph of her mother as a twelve-year-old girl on a beach holiday with her two cousins, Betty and Dolly, holding hands and smiling, captured by her uncle. The sea washes their feet. Years later, her grown-up mother would laugh remembering that happy day. Now both the mother and her laughter are gone, as the mother has been dead nearly twelve years. The poet contrasts the unchanged photographed moment with fast-changing human life. The sea and the smiling girls remain frozen, but real life moves on to death and silence. The poem ends in deep silence — the mother's sweet memory leaves the poet speechless with grief, showing that nothing lasts except memories preserved in a photograph. It teaches that death is final while nature continues unchanged forever.

### 5. Poem Synopsis in Simple English (Stanza-wise)
**Stanza 1:** The poet sees an old photo on cardboard. In it, her mother at age twelve is in the middle with cousins Betty and Dolly on both sides. They hold hands and smile at the camera. Her uncle took the photo during a sea holiday. Small feet are washed by sea water.

**Stanza 2:** Many years later, the grown-up mother laughs when she sees this photo. She remembers the happy sea holiday and the nice clothes they wore. The poet also remembers her mother laughing like this.

**Stanza 3:** Now the mother has been dead nearly twelve years. The poet thinks about the mother's sweet smile. The poet herself has no words now. There is only silence and emptiness. Time has taken away both laughter and life.

### 6. Key Points to Remember for Exam
1. Photo is on cardboard frame.
2. Mother was 12 years old, in middle; Betty and Dolly on sides.
3. They held hands and went for paddling in sea.
4. Uncle took the photo.
5. Mother laughed at photo many years later.
6. Mother dead nearly 12 years ago.
7. Poet is now silent with grief.
8. Main contrast: momentary human life vs permanent nature and photograph.
9. Tone is sad, nostalgic and reflective.
10. Message: Death is final, memories remain.

### 7. Difficult Word Meanings (10 Words)
1. **Cardboard -** thick hard paper used as photo frame
2. **Paddling -** walking in shallow sea water
3. **Transient -** lasting for short time, temporary
4. **Snapshot -** quick photograph
5. **Wry -** sad and twisted smile
6. **Lament -** to feel deep sadness
7. **Silence / Stillness -** complete quiet, no sound
8. **Elegy -** sad poem on death
9. **Nostalgia -** sweet pain of remembering past
10. **Terribly -** very badly, full of fear and sadness

### 8. Important Lines Explained
**1. "The cardboard shows me how it was when the two girl cousins went paddling."**
The poet looks at the old cardboard photo. It shows her mother and cousins enjoying in sea water. The word cardboard shows the photo is very old.

**2. "And the sea, which appears to have changed less, washed their terribly transient feet."**
The sea is still the same after many years, but the girls' feet (their life) were temporary. Girls grew old and died, but nature does not change. This shows human life is short.

**3. "Its silence silences."**
The death of the mother has made everything silent. The memory of her laughter also brings silence now. The poet has no words to express her pain. Grief is too deep for words.

### 9. Five Likely Exam Q&A

**Q1. (Short) What does the photograph show?**
**A:** The photograph shows the poet's mother at twelve years with her cousins Betty and Dolly. They are holding hands and smiling. They are on a beach holiday. The uncle took the photo. Sea water is washing their feet.

**Q2. (Short) What did the mother say about the photo?**
**A:** Years later, the grown-up mother laughed seeing the photo. She remembered the happy day at the sea and their nice clothes. Her laughter shows happy memory of childhood.

**Q3. (Short) What is the meaning of "terribly transient feet"?**
**A:** It means human life is very short and changes fast. The girls' feet were washed by the sea for a moment. Similarly, their childhood and life passed quickly and ended in death.

**Q4. (Long) What is the contrast between the photograph, the sea and human life?**
**A:** The photograph and the sea do not change. The smiling girls in the photo stay the same. The sea still washes the shore. But human life changes fast. The happy twelve-year-old girl became a grown woman and then died. The poet is left alone in silence. So nature is permanent, human life is temporary.

**Q5. (Long) Explain the message of the poem A Photograph.**
**A:** The poem teaches that time does not wait. Happy moments pass. Death takes away loved ones. Only memories and photographs remain. The poet lost her mother nearly twelve years ago and now feels speechless pain. The silence at the end shows deep grief. We should value our loved ones when they are alive, because loss brings only silence.

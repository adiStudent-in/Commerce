# CBSE Class 11 English Hornbill Chapter 1 - Variant 2: Story-Style Super-Friendly Notes
### PROSE: The Portrait of a Lady - Khushwant Singh + POEM: A Photograph - Shirley Toulson

> Hey dost! Darro mat, ye chapter bahut easy aur emotional hai. Socho ek dadi (grandmother) aur uske pota (grandson/author) ki real-life love story hai, plus ek purani photo wali heart-touching poem. Ek baar story ki tarah padh lo, full marks pakke!

---

## PART A: PROSE - The Portrait of a Lady by Khushwant Singh

### 1. Hook Opening - 2 Minute Mein Interest Aa Jayega!

Imagine karo ek itni pyaari dadi jo roz subah tumhe school ke liye taiyaar kare, tumhare saath temple jaye, aur tumhare liye rotiyaan le jaye - kutton ko khilane ke liye!

Ye kahani koi kahani nahi hai, ye author Khushwant Singh ki **sacchi kahani** hai apni dadi ke baare mein. Dadi itni buddhi ho chuki thi ki author ko lagta tha wo hamesha se buddhi hi thi. 20 saal se uski age same lag rahi thi - safed baal, jhurrion wala chehra (wrinkled face), aur haathon mein hamesha mala (rosary of prayer beads).

Lekin is sweet dadi ki life mein ek bada change aaya - gaon (village) se sheher (city). Aur wahi se unka rishta dheere-dheere badal gaya... Last mein aisa emotional end hai ki tum ro padoge. Chalo poori story phase by phase samajhte hain!

### 2. Dadi Kaisi Dikhti Thi? - The Portrait (Yaad Rakhna, Exam Mein Aata Hai!)

Author dadi ko iss tarah describe karta hai:

* **Short, fat, thodi jhuki hui (slightly bent)** - Chalne mein halki si jhuk jati thi.
* **Safed baal (silver white hair)** - Bikhre hue.
* **Jhurrion bhara chehra (wrinkles all over)** - Criss-cross of wrinkles, peaceful and serene like winter landscape in mountains.
* **Hamesha mala japti hui** - Honth (lips) hamesha prayer mein hilte rehte.
* **Sabse sundar cheez?** Uski muskaan aur shaanti. Bina gussa, bina shor.

Author sochta hai dadi bhi hamesha se aisi hi buddhi hongi. Usne dadi ko jawan, pretty kabhi imagine hi nahi kiya.

> Hindi hint: Dadi = Grandmother. Portrait = Tasveer jo yaad dilaye.

### 3. Story in 3 Phases - Gaon, Sheher, Aakhri Din

#### PHASE 1: Village Days - Best Friends Wale Din! (Gaon Ki Life)

Ye sabse pyaara time tha. Author chhota tha, parents sheher chale gaye the, to wo dadi ke saath gaon mein rehta tha. Dadi uski **best friend + mummy** dono thi.

Roz ka routine:
1. **Subah jaldi uthana:** Dadi use jagati, nahlaati, kapde pehnati, lakdi ki takhti (wooden slate) taiyaar karti.
2. **Subah ki prayer:** Syahi (inkpot), kalam lekar dono temple wale school jate. Dadi temple mein mala japti, scriptures padhti, author ko pujari (priest) alphabet + morning prayer sikhata.
3. **Wapsi mein kutton ko roti:** Dadi roz stale chapattiyan (basi rotiyan - chapatti with butter and sugar for breakfast) le jati aur gaon ke kutton ko khilati. Kutte unke peeche poonchh hilate hue aate.

Emotion samjho: Pure pyaar, sukoon, dosti. Dadi khush thi kyunki wo author ko sikha-pa rahi thi - religion, prayer, kindness.

#### PHASE 2: City Days - Dooriyan Badhne Lagi (Sheher Ki Life)

Parents ne dono ko sheher bula liya. Sab badal gaya. Ab dono ek hi kamre mein rehte the, lekin dosti tootne lagi. 3 bade changes hue:

**1. English School:** Ab author English school mein bus se jata tha. Wahan English words, Science, Archimedes principle jaisee cheezen padhai jati thi. God aur scriptures ke baare mein kuch nahi! Dadi ko ye bilkul pasand nahi aaya.

**2. Music ka issue:** Jab author ko music sikhaya jane laga, dadi bahut upset hui. Uske liye music sirf bhikharion (beggars) aur harlots ka kaam tha, shareef gharo ka nahi. Ye uske sanskaron ke khilaaf tha. Wo kuch boli nahi, bas chup ho gayi - silent disapproval.

**3. University Time - Kamra Alag:** Jab author university gaya, use alag kamra mil gaya. Common link of friendship snapped. Dadi ne ise bhi chup-chaap accept kar liya - bina gussa (resigned acceptance). Ab wo akeli ho gayi.

Ab dadi ka naya routine: subah charkha kaatna (spinning wheel), mala japna, aur **chidiyon (sparrows) ko dana khilana.** Aangan mein sainkadon chidiya aati, uske kandhe, sar par baithti. Dadi hasti aur aadha ghanta unke saath khelti. Ye uska happiest half-hour tha.

Emotion samjho: Dadi ko lag raha tha - "mera pota badal raha hai." Lekin usne kabhi roka nahi, bas andar se toot gayi. Ye silent sadness hai.

#### PHASE 3: Last Days - Sabse Emotional Part

**Author videsh (abroad) gaya - 5 saal ke liye:**
Dadi use station chhodne aayi. Wo sentimental nahi hui, royi nahi. Usne author ke maathe (forehead) ko chooma (kissed). continuously prayed. Author ko laga ye unki aakhri mulaqat hai.

**Wapsi - Surprise Celebration:**
5 saal baad jab author wapas aaya, dadi use lene station aayi. Ghar aakar usne pados ki auraton ko bulaya, purana dholak nikala aur **ghanton tak yodhaon ke ghar lautne wale geet gaaye (songs of homecoming of warriors).** Ye pehli baar tha jab dadi ne prayer nahi ki, bas khushi manayi.

**Agli subah - Shant maut (Peaceful Death):**
Overstraining se wo bimar pad gayi - mild fever. Doctor ne bola normal hai, chala jayega. Lekin dadi ne kaha - "Ab mera end paas hai." Bistar par let gayi, mala lekar prayer karne lagi, kisi se baat nahi ki. Shaam ko rosary lifeless fingers se gir gayi - wo shaanti se chal basi (passed away).

**Chidiyon wala moment:**
Hazaaron chidiya kamre mein aayi, dadi ke aas-paas baith gayi. Koi cheen-cheen nahi - bilkul shaant. Author ki mummy ne unke liye roti ke tukde daale, lekin unhone nahi khaye. Jab dadi ka sharir le jaya gaya, wo sab ud gayi. Jaise wo goodbye kehne aayi thi.

> Rula dene wali line: Dadi ne apni poori life prayer mein jee, aur prayer mein hi duniya chhodi.

### 4. Emotions Explained Simply - Feel Karo!

* **Pyaar + Care (Village):** Dadi = Mummy. Full warmth.
* **Dukh + Disappointment (City early):** "Mera pota angrez ban raha hai?" Par boli nahi.
* **Akelepan ko apnana (City later):** Charkha + Sparrows = Naye dost.
* **Khushi ka explosion (Return):** 5 saal ka wait khatam! Dholak bajana = Dil khol ke khushi.
* **Shaanti + Mukti (Death):** Koi darr nahi. Sparrows ka aana = Nature bhi ro raha tha.

### 5. Moral / Lesson - Exam Mein Likho To Topper Lagoge!

1. **Unconditional love:** Dadi ka pyaar kabhi kam nahi hua, chahe pota badal gaya.
2. **Tradition vs Modernity:** Gaon ke sanskar vs Sheher ka English, Science, Music. Balance chahiye.
3. **Respect for elders:** Unki chhoti khushiyan - chidiya, mala - bahut badi hoti hain.
4. **Dignified death:** Shanti se, apne usoolon par jeena aur marna.

### 6. Model Full-Marks Paragraph Answer - Ise Ratt Lo!

**Q: Draw a character sketch of the author's grandmother. (6 Marks)**

> The author's grandmother in 'The Portrait of a Lady' was a short, fat, slightly bent old lady with silver white hair and a deeply wrinkled face. She was deeply religious and always told the beads of her rosary with lips moving in prayer. In the village, she was the author's best friend who woke him up, got him ready for school, accompanied him to the temple-school and fed the village dogs with stale chapattis. In the city, she felt hurt when the author joined an English school where God, scriptures and music were not taught, as she felt music was meant only for beggars and harlots. When the author got a separate room at university and later went abroad for five years, she accepted her loneliness quietly and made sparrows her new companions, feeding them daily. On his return, she celebrated by singing songs of homecoming on an old drum. The next day she fell ill, kept praying and died peacefully, and even the sparrows mourned silently around her body. She symbolises selfless love, tradition and dignity.

Memory Trick: **B-G-C-D-S! Buddhi - Gaon dost - City dukh - Dholak khushi - Sparrow shok.**

## PART B: POEM - A Photograph by Shirley Toulson

### 1. Hook Opening - Ek Purani Photo Ki Kahani!

Poet Shirley Toulson ke paas ek photo hai - uski mummy ki, jab mummy sirf **12 saal ki** thi. Lekin ab mummy iss duniya mein nahi hai. Ye poem **yaadon, muskaan aur khamoshi (silence)** ke baare mein hai.

### 2. Poem in 3 Stages - Past, Past Ka Past, Present

#### STAGE 1: Photo Ka Past - Mummy 12 Saal Ki Thi

* Photo mein teen ladkiyan beach par hain - poet ki mummy (beech mein, sabse badi, 12 saal ki) aur uski do cousins - **Betty aur Dolly.**
* Mummy ke uncle ne photo li thi. Teenon samundar mein paddling ke liye gayi thi.
* Mummy apni chhoti cousin ka haath pakde hai, baal hawa mein ud rahe hain, chehre par sweet smile hai.
* Samundar (sea) unke pair dho raha hai - jo abhi bhi waisa hi hai.

#### STAGE 2: Mummy Ka Past - Mummy Badi Ho Gayi, Photo Par Hasti Thi

* 20-30 saal baad adult mummy yehi photo dekhti hai aur hasti hai.
* Apni beti (poet) ko batati hai - "Dekho Betty-Dolly, dekho humare beach kapde!"
* Sea holiday uska past tha, uski hasi poet ka past ban gayi. Hasi mein thoda dukh bhi hai - wry feeling.

#### STAGE 3: Poet Ka Present - Mummy Ab Nahi Hai

* Ab mummy mar chuki hai - nearly 12 saal ho gaye (jitna wo photo mein jeeyi thi).
* Poet ke paas kehne ko kuch nahi bacha - sirf **khamoshi (silence).**
* Last line: **"Its silence silences"** - maut ki khamoshi poet ko bhi khamosh kar deti hai.

Teenon stage ka funda: **Photo hasti hai, Mummy hasti thi, Poet chup hai.**

### 3. Emotions Explained Simply

* **Bachpan = Laughter:** Carefree, no tension.
* **Middle age = Sweet Memory + Missing:** Hansi ke peeche chhupa dard - laboured ease of loss.
* **Maut ke baad = Silence:** Shabdon se dard bayan nahi hota.

Poet kehna chahti hai: Life bahut chhoti aur badalne wali hai (terribly transient feet). Photo ruk jati hai, insaan chala jata hai. Sea permanent hai, human temporary.

### 4. Moral / Lesson

1. **Time never stops:** Present ko jeeyo.
2. **Loss is part of life:** Yaadein hi reh jati hain.
3. **Photo = Time machine:** Wapas le jati hai, par wapas la nahi sakti.

Connection: Dono mein **yaad, pyaar, aur maut** hai. Dadi ki maut par chidiya chup thi, yahan poet chup hai.

### 5. Model Full-Marks Paragraph Answer - Ise Ratt Lo!

**Q: What is the theme of the poem 'A Photograph'? Explain the three stages. (6 Marks)**

> Shirley Toulson's poem 'A Photograph' deals with the themes of memory, nostalgia, loss and the transience of human life. The poem has three stages. In the first stage, the cardboard photograph shows the poet's mother at twelve with her cousins Betty and Dolly on a beach, holding hands and smiling through flying hair while the sea washes their feet. In the second stage, the grown-up mother laughs while recalling her childhood and looking at the photograph with nostalgia. In the third stage, the mother herself has been dead for nearly twelve years, and the poet recalls her mother's laughter with deep grief. The poet is left speechless and the silence of death silences her. Thus the poem contrasts the permanence of the photograph and the sea with the short, changing nature of human life.

Memory Trick: **P-M-P! Photo (12 saal, beach) - Mummy hansti (yaad) - Poet chup (maut). 12-Betty-Dolly-Beach!**

## QUICK REVISION + VIVA Q&A

### Memory Tricks Summary

1. **Dadi = B-G-C-D-S:** Buddhi look - Gaon (kutte+temple) - City (sparrow+charkha) - Dholak (welcome song) - Sparrow shok (maut par chup).
2. **No-No List:** No God-teaching English school, No Science, No Music!
3. **Poem = 12-B-D-B:** 12 years old, Betty, Dolly, Beach. Fir Mummy Laughs, Fir Poet Silent.
4. **Common word - SILENCE:** Dadi ki maut par chidiya silent, Mummy ki maut par poet silent.
5. **Numbers:** 5 saal abroad gap, Mummy 12 saal ki photo mein, nearly 12 saal se dead.

### Viva-Style Q&A

**Q1. Why did the grandmother disapprove of music and English school?**
A: English school mein God aur scriptures nahi padhaye jate the, sirf English words aur Science tha. Music ko wo beggars/harlots ke liye samajhti thi, shareefon ke liye nahi.

**Q2. How did the grandmother spend her time in the city?**
A: Charkha kaat-ti thi, mala japti thi, aur sparrows ko dana khilati thi. Ye uska favourite happiest half-hour tha.

**Q3. Describe the death of the grandmother and sparrows?**
A: Pote ke lautne par dholak par geet gaaye, mild fever aaya, bola mera end aa gaya, mala japti rahi aur shaanti se mar gayi. Sparrows chup-chaap aayi, roti nahi khai, sharir le jane par ud gayi.

**Q4. Who are the three girls in the photograph?**
A: Beech mein poet ki 12 saal ki mummy, aur cousins Betty aur Dolly. Uncle ne beach holiday par photo li thi.

**Q5. What does 'Its silence silences' mean?**
A: Maut ki khamoshi. Mummy mar chuki hai, ab poet ke paas shabd nahi hain. Maut ka sannata poet ko bhi khamosh kar deta hai. Loss beyond words.

---
> **Last Advice:** Dadi wali story mein **pyaar + sanskar** likhna, Photo wali poem mein **yaad + silence** likhna. Dono mein ek line - death is peaceful but memories remain. Full marks pakke!

# Poem 2 - The Laburnum Top - Practice Questions with Answers
**Poet:** Ted Hughes · **Book:** Hornbill (Class 11) Poem · **Source:** Strictly from Rewritten Notes · Zero hallucination

> How schools ask it: Extract + who is she + comparison basis + device + engine/mask meaning. SQP Extract: sleek as lizard... trembles and thrills. Devices: simile, metaphor, alliteration, imagery.

## Step 1 — Confidence MCQs (10)
**1.** Opening tree is — (a) noisy green (b) silent, quite still, afternoon yellow September sunlight, few leaves yellowing, all seeds fallen (c) spring flowering (d) burning
**Answer:** (b) Laburnum short tree hanging branches, yellow flowers, poisonous seeds; image of quiet barrenness.

**2.** Who breaks silence? — (a) wind (b) goldfinch with twitching chirrup, suddenness-startlement at branch end (c) boy (d) snake
**Answer:** (b) Small singing bird with yellow wing feathers; arrival sudden-startling against stillness.

**3.** She enters — (a) sky (b) thickness where nest hides, sleek as lizard, alert, abrupt (c) river (d) house
**Answer:** (b) Basis sleek-smooth-quick-silent alert motion, not colour; to feed chicks.

**4.** Machine starts up of — (a) smoke (b) chitterings, tremor of wings, trillings (c) bells (d) rain
**Answer:** (b) Chicks' sounds start instantly she slips in; whole tree trembles and thrills.

**5.** Whole tree trembles and thrills means — (a) fear only (b) trembles-thrills fear-joy energy of family life (c) falls (d) sleeps
**Answer:** (b) Alliteration; vibration of life through silent wood.

**6.** Engine of her family means — (a) car (b) tree-with-nest energy source she powers (c) train (d) factory
**Answer:** (b) She starts it on arrival, stokes it full by feeding young; mother powers family system.

**7.** She stokes it full then — (a) dies (b) flirts out to branch-end showing barred face identity mask (c) hides forever (d) cuts tree
**Answer:** (b) Stripe bars on face like mask revealing not hiding identity as she peeks out.

**8.** Farewell sounds are — (a) loud drums (b) eerie delicate whistle-chirrup whisperings (c) silence only (d) thunder
**Answer:** (b) Delicate eerie whisperings as she launches away towards infinite open sky.

**9.** Ending is — (a) stays noisy (b) laburnum subsides to empty, same silent-still as beginning (c) tree flowers (d) nest falls
**Answer:** (b) Circle silence-life-silence; visit brief vibrant interruption; departure felt as loss.

**10.** Dominant colour-theme — (a) red war (b) yellow unifies sunlight-leaves-flowers-feathers September; theme interdependence-motherhood (c) blue sea (d) black night
**Answer:** (b) Silent tree alive only with bird; arrival energy, departure emptiness.

## Step 2 — School Extracts RTC (2 sets x 3)
### Set 1 — SQP Exact
> "Then sleek as a lizard, and alert, and abrupt, She enters the thickness, and a machine starts up Of chitterings, and a tremor of wings, and trillings — The whole tree trembles and thrills."

**Q1. Who is she and to what is she compared? (1)**
**Answer:** Goldfinch mother-bird; compared to lizard — sleek, alert, abrupt in movement.

**Q2. What causes machine to start? (1)**
**Answer:** Her slipping into thick foliage where nest hides; chicks' chitterings-wing tremor-trillings start; tree is engine of family she stokes full feeding.

**Q3. Device in sleek as a lizard? (1)**
**Answer:** Simile with explicit as — compares manner quick-smooth-silent; machine-engine is metaphor for family energy; trembles-thrills alliteration.

### Set 2 — Opening + Closing
> "The Laburnum top is silent, quite still In the afternoon yellow September sunlight, A few leaves yellowing, all its seeds fallen."
> "Then with eerie delicate whistle-chirrup whisperings She launches away, towards the infinite And the laburnum subsides to empty."

**Q1. What do you notice about beginning and ending? (1)**
**Answer:** Both silent-still; opens still yellow light, closes subsides to empty — circle with brief life between.

**Q2. What breaks silence and how does it leave? (1)**
**Answer:** Goldfinch comes with twitching chirrup suddenness-startlement; leaves with eerie delicate whistle-chirrup whisperings towards infinite sky.

**Q3. Why is September-yellow significant? (1)**
**Answer:** Yellow sunlight, yellowing leaves, yellow flowers-feathers show ripeness-decay; stillness makes bird's energy stand out; dominant colour unifies tree-bird.

## Step 3 — Short Answers 40-50 words (6)
**Q1. Compare beginning and ending of poem.**
**Answer:** Both are silent and still. Opening shows yellow September light with yellowing leaves, seeds fallen. Ending shows laburnum subsiding to empty after bird launches to infinite. Visit is brief vibrant interruption proving life visits but does not stay.

**Q2. To what is bird's movement compared and on what basis?**
**Answer:** To lizard — sleek as lizard, alert, abrupt. Basis is manner not looks: quick, smooth, silent, watchful motion slipping into thickness. Both show wild alertness protecting young, making suddenness-startlement vivid against still tree.

**Q3. Why is image of engine evoked?**
**Answer:** Tree-with-nest is engine of her family. Arrival starts machine of chitterings, tremor, trillings; she stokes it full feeding chicks. Industrial metaphor captures maternal power turning silent wood into living system; without her engine stops, trembles-thrills cease.

**Q4. What does barred face identity mask mean?**
**Answer:** Stripe-like bars on goldfinch face act like mask that reveals not hides identity. Peeking from branch-end before launching to infinite, she declares species-self. Identity survives concealment; mother shows briefly between duties inside thickness and departure.

**Q5. How do sound words prove interdependence?**
**Answer:** Alone tree silent-still. With bird come twitching chirrup, chitterings, tremor, trillings, trembles-thrills, whistle-whisperings. Sounds start only on entering thickness, stop on launching infinite. Tree gives shelter-nest; bird brings voice-life; neither lives fully alone.

**Q6. What is theme of poem?**
**Answer:** Interdependence and motherhood. Silent laburnum bursts alive when goldfinch arrives to feed chicks, then subsides empty when she flies to infinite. Arrival brings energy, departure emptiness. Yellow unifies sun-leaves-feathers, showing stillness needs life to mean peace.

## Step 4 — Long Answers 120-150 words (3)
**Q1. How does poem move in circle from silence to silence through goldfinch?**
**Answer:** Poem opens silent-quite still in afternoon yellow September sunlight, few leaves yellowing, all seeds fallen — laburnum short tree with hanging yellow flowers facing barrenness. Silence breaks with goldfinch twitching chirrup, suddenness-startlement at branch end. Sleek as lizard, alert-abrupt, she enters thickness where nest hides, starting machine of chitterings, tremor of wings, trillings; whole tree trembles and thrills. It is engine of her family she stokes full feeding, then flirts to branch-end showing barred face identity mask. Then with eerie delicate whistle-chirrup whisperings she launches towards infinite open sky, and laburnum subsides to empty exactly as began. Circle shows life as brief burst between stillnesses; departure makes stillness feel void, proving interdependence where tree offers shelter and mother brings energy, unified by yellow light-feathers-leaves.

**Q2. Explain machine-engine image and mask.**
**Answer:** When sleek-alert-abrupt goldfinch enters thickness, machine starts up — chitterings, tremor, trillings — whole tree trembles and thrills. Tree-with-nest is engine of her family: like engine needs fuel, family needs feeding; she stokes it full then flirts out showing barred face identity mask. Industrial metaphor turns silent wood into living system powered by maternal energy, not metal. Trembles suggests shaking fear-vibration, thrills joy-excitement — alliteration capturing both. Mask is stripe bars on face peeking at branch-end: not hiding but revealing identity before eerie delicate farewell to infinite. Without starter-stoker, engine stops and subsides empty. Hughes thus honours motherhood as vital force: small yellow-wing singer transforms yellowing September stillness into thrill, proving silent support becomes alive only when love enters and labours.

**Q3. What role do yellow, stillness and sounds play?**
**Answer:** Yellow dominates — afternoon sunlight, yellowing leaves, laburnum flowers, goldfinch feathers, September ripeness-decay — unifying tree and bird in one glow where movement stands out. Stillness frames poem: silent-quite still opening, subsides empty closing; seeds fallen signals barren quiet awaiting life. Sounds mark visit: twitching chirrup announces suddenness-startlement, chitterings-tremor-trillings power machine-engine, trembles-thrills vibrates whole tree, eerie delicate whistle-chirrup whisperings softens farewell to infinite. Alone silence is barren; with bird it thrills. Tree gives thickness-shelter, bird gives voice-life — interdependence. Lizard simile stresses sleek alert abrupt entry; barred mask stresses identity flash. Moral: motherhood ignites family engine, departure restores stillness now felt as loss, teaching ecology where stillness needs life to mean peace and life needs stillness to be heard.

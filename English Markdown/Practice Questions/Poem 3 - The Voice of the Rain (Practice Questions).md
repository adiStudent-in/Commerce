# Poem 3 - The Voice of the Rain - Practice Questions with Answers
**Poet:** Walt Whitman · **Book:** Hornbill (Class 11) Poem · **Source:** Strictly from Rewritten Notes · Zero hallucination

> How schools ask it: Dialogue form, who speaks, Poem of Earth meaning, water cycle match, brackets why, opposites. Devices: personification, metaphor, imagery, paradox. SQP Short asks eternal lifecycle earth to sky.

## Step 1 — Confidence MCQs (10)
**1.** Two voices are — (a) two men (b) poet's and rain's (c) rain-cloud (d) poet alone
**Answer:** (b) Poet asks And who art thou to soft-falling shower; strange to tell gave answer as here translated.

**2.** Strange to tell means — (a) familiar (b) surprisingly-unbelievably shower answered (c) sad (d) loud
**Answer:** (b) Rain never speaks yet reply comes in translation; personification makes science intimate.

**3.** I am — (a) king (b) Poem of Earth (c) river only (d) cloud only
**Answer:** (b) Nature's own song; eternal selfless giver beautifying origin.

**4.** Eternal I rise — (a) touchable (b) impalpable out of land and bottomless sea upward to heaven (c) downwards (d) sideways
**Answer:** (b) Impalpable means untouchable as vapour; evaporation from land-sea.

**5.** Vaguely form'd changed yet same means — (a) destroyed (b) clouds form, form shifts yet identity stays (c) disappears (d) splits
**Answer:** (b) Upward to heaven changed in form yet somehow same — condensation paradox.

**6.** I descend to — (a) destroy (b) lave droughts, atomies, dust-layers of globe (c) sleep (d) hide
**Answer:** (b) Lave means wash-bathe; atomies tiny particles; precipitation washing earth.

**7.** Without me seeds were — (a) grown (b) seeds only, latent, unborn (c) dead stones (d) flowers
**Answer:** (b) Latent means hidden-dormant; rain awakens hidden life, revives growth.

**8.** Forever by day-night I — (a) rest (b) give back life to own origin, make pure and beautify it (c) leave (d) dry
**Answer:** (b) Ceaseless unpaid reciprocity: receive-transform-return beautified; water cycle poetically stated.

**9.** Bracket lines say — (a) mistake (b) song issuing birth-place after fulfilment wandering reck'd-unreck'd duly love returns (c) title (d) footnote
**Answer:** (b) Universal aside beyond rain; reck'd heeded, unreck'd ignored; rain:earth :: song:soul.

**10.** Opposites holding cycle — (a) none (b) rise-fall, land-sea, day-night, reck'd-unreck'd, form'd-formless (c) colours only (d) sounds only
**Answer:** (b) Eternity in alternation; change keeps identity, neglect stops not love.

## Step 2 — School Extracts RTC (2 sets x 3)
### Set 1 — Rain's Reply Exact
> "I am the Poem of Earth, said the voice of the rain, Eternal I rise impalpable out of the land and the bottomless sea, Upward to heaven, whence, vaguely form'd, altogether changed, and yet the same, I descend to lave the droughts, atomies, dust-layers of the globe, And all that in them without me were seeds only, latent, unborn;"

**Q1. Why does rain call itself Poem of Earth? (1)**
**Answer:** Like poem rhythmic-beautiful-meaningful returning to origin; earth's expression beautifying source selflessly, nature's song.

**Q2. How does this match water cycle? (1)**
**Answer:** Rise impalpable = evaporation vapour from land-bottomless sea; vaguely form'd = condensation clouds; changed yet same = form shift; descend lave = precipitation washing droughts-atomies-dust.

**Q3. Why mention droughts-atomies-seeds together? (1)**
**Answer:** Vast dryness to minute particles to hidden life — all need rain; without it remain latent unborn seeds only, proving life-giver not mere weather.

### Set 2 — Origin + Song Exact
> "And who art thou? said I to the soft-falling shower, Which, strange to tell, gave me an answer, as here translated:"
> "(For song, issuing from its birth-place, after fulfilment, wandering Reck'd or unreck'd, duly with love returns.)"

**Q1. Who speaks and what is strange? (1)**
**Answer:** Poet asks soft-falling shower who art thou; rain answers. Strange to tell = surprisingly-unbelievably, shower cannot speak yet translated reply comes.

**Q2. Why are last two lines in brackets? (1)**
**Answer:** General truth aside beyond rain's reply. Poet adds general truth: song issuing birth-place after fulfilment wandering reck'd-unreck'd duly love returns. It applies cycle to music-poetry-art, showing rain:earth as song:heart, eternal reciprocity regardless heeded or ignored.

**Q3. What parallel is drawn? (1)**
**Answer:** Rain:earth :: song:soul. Both rise-issue, fulfil-wander, return-purify. Forever day-night give back life; art returns beautifying maker regardless notice.

## Step 3 — Short Answers 40-50 words (6)
**Q1. How does rain describe eternal lifecycle earth to sky?**
**Answer:** Rain is Poem of Earth rising eternal impalpable vapour from land-bottomless sea upward to heaven, vaguely formed changed yet same, descending to lave droughts-atomies-dust, awakening latent unborn seeds, forever day-night giving back life, purifying-beautifying origin.

**Q2. What does strange to tell signify?**
**Answer:** It means surprisingly-unbelievably. Poet asks soft-falling shower who art thou, and shower answers in translation. It admits fantasy inviting belief; personification turns water-cycle science into intimate imagined dialogue between poet's voice and rain's voice.

**Q3. Why Poem of Earth?**
**Answer:** Rain calls itself earth's poem — rhythmic, beautiful, meaningful, returning to origin. Like song it rises, fulfils, wanders, returns with love. It gives back life to land-sea source, purifies-beautifies ceaselessly, proving selfless eternal service, not mere weather.

**Q4. How do droughts-atomies-seeds prove life-giver?**
**Answer:** Droughts show vast dryness, atomies tiny particles, dust-layers globe surface, seeds latent unborn hidden life. Together they span large-minute-dormant, all needing wash-awakening. Without rain they stay seeds only; with lave-descend they revive, proving universal nourisher.

**Q5. Why brackets for song lines?**
**Answer:** Brackets mark universal aside beyond rain's reply. Poet adds general truth: song issuing birth-place after fulfilment wandering reck'd-unreck'd duly love returns. It applies cycle to music-poetry-art, showing rain:earth as song:heart, eternal reciprocity regardless heeded or ignored.

**Q6. What opposites balance poem?**
**Answer:** Rise-fall, land-sea-bottomless-heaven, vaguely formed-changed yet same, day-night, reck'd-unreck'd, form'd-formless. Contraries hold cycle proving change keeps identity. Eternity lives in alternation: neglect stops not love, descent follows rise, beautifying return completes circle.

## Step 4 — Long Answers 120-150 words (3)
**Q1. Explain rain's lifecycle and how it matches water cycle.**
**Answer:** Asked And who art thou, soft-falling shower strangely answers: I am Poem of Earth. Eternal it rises impalpable untouchable vapour out of land and bottomless sea upward to heaven, vaguely formed altogether changed yet same — evaporation then condensation where form shifts but essence stays. Then it descends to lave wash droughts, atomies tiny particles, dust-layers of globe — precipitation cleansing earth. All without it were seeds only, latent hidden unborn — dormancy awaiting water. Forever by day and night it gives back life to own origin, making pure and beautifying — growth and return enriching source that sent vapour. Science evaporation-cloud-rain-growth-return is poetically stated as selfless eternal service. Rain gives ceaselessly, purifies what bore it, proving nature's song returns beautified, unlike human expecting return, completing circle where origin is both giver and receiver.

**Q2. Explain rain-song parallel in bracketed lines.**
**Answer:** After describing Poem of Earth cycle, poet adds in brackets: For song issuing from birth-place after fulfilment wandering Reck'd or unreck'd duly with love returns. Brackets signal universal aside beyond rain's voice — general truth applied to music-poetry-art. Song born in heart-soul, fulfilled in world, wanders heeded or ignored, yet duly returns with love to maker, just as rain rises-fulfils-wanders-returns purifying land-sea origin. Both complete circle enriching source regardless notice: reck'd heeded or unreck'd unheeded stops not love. Rain:earth :: song:soul. Opposites rise-descend, land-heaven, day-night balance movement proving identity persists through change. Moral is selfless eternal reciprocity — receive-transform-return beautified. Art like rain dignifies creator, forever day-night giving back life, making pure. Poet thus universalises water cycle into aesthetics: creativity must return to beautify birth-place.

**Q3. Why is rain called Poem of Earth and what values does it teach?**
**Answer:** Rain calls itself Poem of Earth — earth's rhythmic beautiful meaningful expression, nature's song. It rises eternal impalpable from land-bottomless sea to heaven, vaguely formed changed yet same, descends to lave droughts-atomies-dust, awakens latent unborn seeds, forever day-night gives back life to origin purifying-beautifying. Like poem it has rhythm-movement-meaning-return. Values are selfless eternal service, reciprocity, purity. Origin sends vapour; rain returns same source giving-cleansing unpaid ceaseless, unlike humans expecting reward. It serves vast droughts and minute atomies alike, ignoring scale, reviving hidden potential. Song parallel extends lesson: art after fulfilment wandering reck'd-unreck'd duly love returns, dignifying poet. Both teach receive-transform-return beautified, change keeping identity, neglect not stopping love. Strange to tell dialogue makes science intimate, showing ecology and creativity share circle where giving completes life and beautifies home.

## Step 1 — 10 Confidence MCQs

**1.** Address —
(a) 46, Marconi Street, Mrs Dorling (b) 64 Lane (c) 19 Street (d) Unknown villa
**Answer:** (a) — Given by mother with Remember that; broad back, brown coat, shapeless hat lifting suitcase.

**2.** First-visit proof —
(a) Letter (b) Mother's green knitted cardigan, wooden buttons pale from washing, worn by Dorling opening door chink denying No, I don't know you
(c) Photo (d) Key
**Answer:** (b) — Narrator: I'm Mrs S's daughter; reply Have you come back? Thought no one come back; not convenient, shut.

**3.** Dorling took —
(a) Nothing (b) Table-silver one go, antique plates wall, large vases, crockery each trip; herself suggested-insisted to save if leave
(c) Books only (d) Money
**Answer:** (b) — Old acquaintance unseen years renewed; mother reprovingly defended; marvelled risk full suitcase; crick back.

**4.** Gap feeling —
(a) Greedy joy (b) After Liberation first not interested, afraid confronting connection gone, cupboards-boxes waiting vain; later normal bread lighter, sleep safe, curiosity to see-touch-remember
(c) Immediate suit (d) Forgot at once
**Answer:** (b) — Trauma avoidance turning to recovery curiosity, not possession hunger.

**5.** Second-visit opener —
(a) Dorling welcomed (b) Girl about fifteen opened, mother on errand; passage Hanukkah candle-holder never used cumbersome vs single candlestick
(c) Empty house (d) Police
**Answer:** (b) — Entry to living-room knew and did not know, tasteless ugly muggy smell.

**6.** Cloth proof —
(a) New cloth (b) Woollen table-cloth rubbed, burn mark edge never repaired
(c) Silk (d) Cardigan
**Answer:** (b) — Personal memory marker proving family ownership in strange surroundings.

**7.** Tea objects —
(a) Plastic (b) White pot gold border lid, spoons antique box lots more, apple on pewter plate loved as child, wall plates eaten off wasn't special
(c) No tea (d) Hotel set
**Answer:** (b) — Girl casual misuse shows heirlooms stripped of love, tasteless display.

**8.** Silver anecdote + flight —
(a) Stole silver (b) Never knew everyday cutlery silver till mother asked polishing help; drawer opened to show theirs; fled I must catch train amid jingling spoons-forks
(c) Bought silver (d) Stayed dinner
**Answer:** (b) — Strange voice memory; jingle drives flight, seeing enough.

**9.** Resolve —
(a) Take back (b) Forget address, easiest of all forget; objects lose value severed strange surroundings
(c) Sue (d) Move in
**Answer:** (b) — Corner confirmed correct yet no wish remember; healing not betrayal.

**10.** Rented room limit —
(a) Palace (b) Small rented room, shreds black-out paper windows, barely handful cutlery fits narrow drawer, cannot hold past rituals
(c) Big house (d) Museum
**Answer:** (b) — Material return impossible; mother lives in heart, not vases.

## Step 3 — 6 Short Answers (40-50 words)

**Q1. What does Have you come back reveal?**
**Answer:** Reveals Holocaust persecution-deportation background. Dorling never expected survival; thought no one come back. Mother-household gone, only daughter returns. Guilt-tinged surprise explains chink opening, denial, inconvenient shut to protect plundered holdings.

**Q2. Saving or plundering by Dorling?**
**Answer:** Mask of saving covers greed. Herself suggested-insisted, each trip full suitcase — silver one go, plates, vases causing crick back. Wore cardigan, denied orphan, let daughter misuse antiques everyday. Exploited danger, possessively keeping without valuing.

**Q3. Why afraid then curious after Liberation?**
**Answer:** At first not interested, even afraid confronting cupboards-boxes waiting vain for connection gone. As normalcy returned — bread lighter, sleep unthreatened, familiar view — recovery curiosity woke to see-touch-remember, not greed, marking grief seeking.

**Q4. Describe second-visit room.**
**Answer:** Fifteen-year-old admits; Hanukkah cumbersome in passage. Living-room knew-did not know — family things tasteless amid ugly furniture, muggy smell. Burn cloth, gold-border pot, pewter apple, wall plates everyday use, silver drawer jingle force flight for train.

**Q5. Why pain not comfort?**
**Answer:** Scattered tasteless, stripped of mother-love-setting, same cloth warms memory but chills stranger room. Burn, gold pot, pewter apple remind loss. Instead of reviving warmth they prove severance — seeing enough, taking would prolong ache.

**Q6. Why forget easiest?**
**Answer:** Corner confirmed correct address yet no wish remember. Linked objects instantly lose value severed strange surroundings. Small rented room with black-out paper, narrow drawer cannot house rituals. Forgetting is healing survival, carrying mother in heart.

## Step 4 — 3 Long Answers (120-150 words)

**Q1. Justify title The Address. OR War destroyed trust-warmth?**
**Answer:** Entire story pivots on scrap — 46, Marconi Street, Remember that from mother lifting broad-back brown-coat shapeless-hat suitcase. First visit admits hope through chink-green-cardigan proof yet shuts with Have you come back, not convenient, bay-window peep. Second delivers pain — fifteen-year-old, Hanukkah cumbersome, burn cloth, gold-border pot, pewter apple, wall plates everyday, silver jingle flight for train. Corner confirms correct yet wishes not remember: linked objects lose value severed surroundings; rented black-out narrow-drawer room cannot hold large past. Forgetting easiest is healing, mother in heart not vases, clinging to letting go. OR War cost human not material — silver survives mothers annihilated; Dorling's saving masks plunder; cold reception, tasteless misuse destroy trust-warmth; memory must live within.

**Q2. Sketch Mrs Dorling. OR How treated narrator and why?**
**Answer:** Opportunistic greedy exploiter wearing helpful mask. Old acquaintance unseen years, suddenly renews in first half War, each visit takes something — silver one go, antique plates, large vases, crockery straining crick back — herself suggesting-insisting to save if leave, risking full suitcase. Mother reprovingly defends. After War wears green cardigan pale buttons, opens chink, denies I'm Mrs S's daughter with Have you come back, shuts as inconvenient. Lets daughter treat heirlooms junk — lots more spoons, wall plates everyday. Neither understands nor values, possessively keeps. OR Cold rude from guilt-greed: no recognition, no invite, peep curtain, errand absence second time; acknowledging daughter risks return, so denial and casual display protect possession.

**Q3. Second visit turning point. OR Significance of burn, gold border, pewter, jingle?**
**Answer:** Let in by fifteen-year-old during errand, narrator enters knew-did not know room — tasteless ugly muggy. Hanukkah cumbersome unused, woollen cloth burn never repaired rubbed for proof, white pot gold border pours tea, antique-box spoons lots more, pewter apple loved as child over tea-table, wall plates eaten off not special, silver everyday cutlery revelation till polishing help recalled. OR Each marker authenticates ownership yet proves death — burn unrepaired homely, gold border ritual tea, pewter apple childhood still-life, jingle everyday cheapening; warmth in memory chills in misuse, so leaving behind frees.

# Ch 3 - Discovering Tut - Practice Questions with Answers
**Author:** A. R. Williams · **Book:** Hornbill (Class 11) Prose · **Source:** Strictly from Rewritten Notes · Zero hallucination

> How schools ask this chapter: Prose Extract RTC 1x3, Short Answer 40-50 words (3 marks), Long Answer 120-150 words (6 marks), MCQs 1 mark. SQP pattern: Extract 3x1, Short 40-50 words, Long 120-150 words.

## Step 1 — Confidence MCQs (10)
**1.** Tut was — (a) old king (b) teenager, last heir of powerful family ruling centuries, dead 3,300 years, laden with gold (c) farmer (d) priest
**Answer:** (b) Forgotten till tomb discovery in 1922; murder most extreme speculation; how died, how old remain questions.

**2.** Howard Carter in 1922 found — (a) empty cave (b) richest royal collection ever found, hastily ransacked yet complete (c) modern city (d) only stones
**Answer:** (b) British archaeologist after futile search; gold meant to guarantee resurrection beside board games, bronze razor, linen, food-wine.

**3.** Tut was stuck because — (a) water (b) ritual resins hardened, cementing Tut to bottom of solid gold coffin (c) ropes (d) ice
**Answer:** (b) Even baking in blazing sunshine at 149 degrees Fahrenheit for hours did not budge it.

**4.** To free the body Carter's men — (a) used water (b) chiselled consolidated material from beneath limbs-trunk, removed head, severed nearly every major joint (c) left it (d) melted gold
**Answer:** (b) Reassembled on sand layer in padded wooden box; Hawass says very bad condition because of what Carter did in the 1920s.

**5.** 1968 X-ray revealed — (a) perfect body (b) breast-bone and front ribs missing beneath resin caking chest (c) gold heart (d) nothing new
**Answer:** (b) By anatomy professor, 40+ years after discovery; startling fact deepening mystery of premature death.

**6.** CT night was — (a) 1922 (b) 6 p.m. 5 January 2005, Valley of the Kings, first time leaving tomb in almost 80 years (c) 1968 (d) 2003
**Answer:** (b) Cramped rock-cut tomb 26 feet underground; tourists gazed at murals and gilded face; pharaoh's curse whispered.

**7.** CT result — (a) 10 images (b) 1,700 digital X-ray cross-sections, head in 0.62 mm slices with eerie detail (c) failed (d) one photo
**Answer:** (b) Hundreds of slices like bread into 3-D virtual body; clear neck vertebrae, hand, rib cage, skull transection for forensic reconstruction.

**8.** The "curse" joke happened because — (a) lights failed (b) sand clogged cooler fan, million-dollar scanner quit; substitute fans worked; guard joked "Curse of the pharaoh" (c) mummy fell (d) rain came
**Answer:** (b) Workmen carried Tut by hydraulic lift to scanner trailer; finished in less than three hours; Hawass relieved: "now I think I will go and sleep."

**9.** Scanner donated by — (a) Carter (b) National Geographic Society and Siemens, for Egyptian Mummy Project counting almost 600 since late 2003 (c) school (d) temple
**Answer:** (b) Tut one of first scanned — in death as in life moving regally ahead of countrymen.

**10.** Family drama + ending — (a) pyramids only (b) Amenhotep IV-Akhenaten promoted Aten sun disk, moved Thebes to Amarna, smashed Amun images-closed temples; Tutankhaten became Tutankhamun living image of Amun, reigned 9 years; Orion soul of Osiris watched above tomb (c) no change (d) Tut lived long
**Answer:** (b) Ray Johnson called Akhenaten wacky; Smenkhkare briefly came-vanished; Tut restored old ways, died unexpectedly; funeral death rattle of dynasty.

## Step 2 — School Extracts RTC (2 sets x 3)
### Set 1 — Carter
> "Ritual resins had hardened, cementing Tut to the bottom of his solid gold coffin. Carter baked the mummy in blazing sunshine at 149 degrees Fahrenheit for hours — nothing budged — and finally reported with scientific detachment that the consolidated material 'had to be chiselled away from beneath the limbs and trunk.'"

**Q1. What cemented Tut and why could it not be moved? (1)**
**Answer:** Hardened ritual resins cemented him to solid gold coffin; even 149°F sunshine for hours did not loosen it.

**Q2. What drastic step followed and what was its effect? (1)**
**Answer:** Material chiselled from beneath limbs-trunk; men removed head, severed nearly every major joint, reassembled on sand in padded box — very bad condition.

**Q3. Was there any defence for Carter? (1)**
**Answer:** Yes — with scientific detachment he had little choice; otherwise thieves would have circumvented guards and ripped mummy apart for gold adornments.

### Set 2 — CT Night
> "At 6 p.m. on 5 January 2005, in the ancient cemetery called the Valley of the Kings, the world's most famous mummy glided head first into a CT scanner — leaving his tomb for the first time in almost 80 years... 1,700 digital X-ray images in cross section, Tut's head scanned in 0.62 millimetre slices... 'Curse of the pharaoh,' joked a guard nervously."

**Q1. When-where did the scan happen and why was it historic? (1)**
**Answer:** 5 Jan 2005, Valley of the Kings; Tut left tomb first time in almost 80 years to probe lingering medical mysteries of teenager dead 3,300 years.

**Q2. What interrupted the scan and what did it cause? (1)**
**Answer:** Sand clogged cooler fan, million-dollar scanner quit; two men brought white plastic substitute fans; guard nervously joked pharaoh's curse.

**Q3. What was the technical outcome? (1)**
**Answer:** 1,700 cross-section images into 3-D virtual body; head 0.62 mm slices with eerie detail; clear vertebrae, hand, rib cage, skull for forensic reconstruction; back in tomb within three hours.

## Step 3 — Short Answers 40-50 words (6)
**Q1. Why has Tut's body faced repeated scrutiny?**
**Answer:** Tut was teenager last heir of centuries-old family, dead 3,300 years amid murder speculation. Funeral was death rattle of dynasty yet particulars unclear. 1968 missing breast-bone-ribs deepened mystery, so 2005 CT sought age and cause for forensic reconstruction.

**Q2. Why was Carter's investigation criticised yet defended?**
**Answer:** Resins cemented Tut to gold coffin; 149°F sunshine failed. Men chiselled material, removed head, severed nearly every major joint, leaving very bad condition per Hawass. Defence: no legitimate force could move it; thieves would otherwise circumvent guards and rip body for gold.

**Q3. What did the 1968 X-ray reveal and why was it important?**
**Answer:** Anatomy professor X-rayed mummy revealing missing breast-bone and front ribs beneath chest resin. It was startling because it deepened premature-death mystery. It shifted focus from treasure to life-death truth, raising twin questions how Tut died and how old he was.

**Q4. Describe the CT night and curse joke.**
**Answer:** On 5 Jan 2005 Tut was carried like pallbearers up ramp-stairs by hydraulic lift to National Geographic-Siemens trailer. Sand clogged cooler fan, scanner quit, guard joked curse. Substitute fans finished 1,700 images, 0.62 mm head slices, and Tut rested back within three hours.

**Q5. Why did Ray Johnson call Akhenaten wacky and how did Tut reverse him?**
**Answer:** Amenhotep IV promoted Aten sun disk, renamed Akhenaten servant of Aten, moved Thebes to Amarna, smashed Amun images and closed temples. After Smenkhkare vanished, Tutankhaten became Tutankhamun living image of Amun, restoring old ways, reigning about nine years.

**Q6. What does the Orion ending suggest?**
**Answer:** After scan wind stopped, winter air lay cold-still like death in valley of departed. Above entrance stood Orion, Egyptians' soul of Osiris god of afterlife watching boy king. It shows myth meeting science: technology probes while cosmos guards, letting Tut rest in peace.

## Step 4 — Long Answers 120-150 words (3)
**Q1. How has modern science helped unravel Tut's mysteries from Carter to CT?**
**Answer:** Tut, teenager last heir laden with gold, lay forgotten till Howard Carter found richest royal collection ever in 1922. To free resin-cemented body from solid gold coffin after 149°F sunshine failed, Carter chiselled material, removed head and severed nearly every major joint, saving gold meant to guarantee resurrection but leaving very bad condition. Archaeology then moved treasure to truth. 1968 X-ray found missing breast-bone and front ribs under chest resin, deepening twin questions how he died and how old he was. Breakthrough came 5 Jan 2005 in Valley of Kings when portable National Geographic-Siemens CT made 1,700 cross-sections, 0.62 mm head slices with eerie detail, clear vertebrae-hand-rib-skull for forensic reconstruction. Despite sand-blocked fan curse joke, Tut returned within three hours, with Orion soul of Osiris watching, showing science seeks truth while respecting dead.

**Q2. Justify Carter's actions and evaluate their cost.**
**Answer:** Carter discovered Tut in 1922 after futile search, recording funerary treasures for months — stunning gold for resurrection beside board games, bronze razor, linen, food-wine, three nested coffins and shroud with willow-olive, celery, lotus, cornflowers. But ritual resins hardened, cementing body to gold coffin. With scientific detachment he reported consolidated material had to be chiselled from limbs-trunk. His men removed head, severed nearly every major joint, reassembling on sand in padded box where Tut rests. Hawass condemns very bad condition. Yet defence stands: no legitimate force could move it, and thieves would circumvent guards to rip mummy for collars, necklaces, bracelets, rings, amulets, apron, sandals, sheaths, mask. He saved richest collection from ransack. Cost was bodily damage now probed by X-ray and CT. It teaches curiosity must balance preservation, as later Egyptian Mummy Project scanning 600 mummies proves.

**Q3. Explain the royal family drama and why Tut's funeral was death rattle of dynasty.**
**Answer:** Amenhotep III ruled almost four decades at golden height. Son Amenhotep IV promoted Aten sun disk, renamed Akhenaten, moved religious capital Thebes to Akhetaten-Amarna, attacked major god Amun smashing images and closing temples. Ray Johnson called it horrific wacky time as centuries-old family ended. After Akhenaten died, mysterious Smenkhkare briefly appeared-exited traceless. Then very young Tutankhaten took throne, renamed Tutankhamun living image of Amun, restoring old ways and reigning about nine years before unexpected death. As last heir, his demise ended line; hence funeral was death rattle of dynasty — big event even by royal standards yet particulars unclear. That is why murder speculation persists, tourists shiver at curse 26 feet underground, and 5 Jan 2005 CT with sand-fan joke and Orion watching seeks closure where history left silence.

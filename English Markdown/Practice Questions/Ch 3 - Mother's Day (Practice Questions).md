# Ch 3 - Mother's Day - Practice Questions with Answers
**Author:** J.B. Priestley · **Book:** Snapshots Play (Class 11) · **Source:** Strictly from Rewritten Notes · Zero hallucination

> How schools ask: Character contrast, what said to each, swap justified, vanity punctured, ending, drama as message. Long 120-150 words, Short 40-50, Extract 1x4.

## Step 1 — 10 Confidence MCQs

**1.** Setting-characters —
(a) Pearson small semi-detached villa London suburb, autumn afternoon; Annie pleasant worried forties flurried; Fitzgerald older heavier sinister deep smokes
(b) Village hut (c) Palace (d) School
**Answer:** (a) — Spoilt Doris pretty early twenties, Cyril masculine, George fifty decent solemn pompous; Fitzgerald real fortune-teller.

**2.** Fitzgerald past —
(a) Teacher (b) 12 years out East learnt cards-tricks; husband rose Lieutenant Quartermaster
(c) London clerk (d) Actress
**Answer:** (b) — Heavy sinister personality, strong decisive, supplies courage Pearson lacks.

**3.** Fortune advice —
(a) Divorce (b) Put foot down, mistress house boss family; orders servant nightly enjoyment stays home; spoilt ruin them you
(c) Leave (d) Cry
**Answer:** (b) — Could be good-bad depends now; hints fond hates unpleasantness cannot confront.

**4.** Swap device —
(a) Costumes (b) Occult chant Arshtatta dum-lam-lamdumbona; bold in Pearson body, timid alarmed next door; They'll have me deal won't know
(c) Dream (d) Letter
**Answer:** (b) — Only shock wakes deaf family; fantasy social surgery, later restoration with affection.

**5.** To Doris —
(a) Pampers (b) No tea Yours isn't, may eat Clarendon square meal; mocks yellow silk ironing, Charlie Spence buck teeth half-witted, somebody better; tears
(c) Gifts (d) Ignores
**Answer:** (b) — Retort twice hours no wages thanks; exposes choice.

**6.** To Cyril —
(a) Serves (b) Couldn't bother tea, dislike mending; joined movement Changes; eight-hour claim turned forty-hour week, two days off weekend, favour bed-cook if asked nicely thanked
(c) Praises (d) Pays
**Answer:** (b) — Whispering giggling pair taught Union logic against them; may go weekend herself.

**7.** To George —
(a) Praised (b) Stout at hour shocks; tea unwanted tell club bar; special snooker night mocked; Pompy-ompy slow pompous nightly leaving wife; Cyril yes Dad afraid confirms; stagger hit head
(c) Promoted (d) Paid
**Answer:** (b) — Public mockery private lacks; punctures solemn self-importance.

**8.** Manners + insult words —
(a) Polite thanks (b) Cyril calling silly old bag silenced as silly spoilt young piecan; George warned slap big fat silly face if shouts; clot-barmy slang
(c) Ignored (d) Left
**Answer:** (b) — Lecture on manners to guests; firm hand without aggression.

**9.** Swap-back advice —
(a) Apologise (b) Cannot bear misery, swap back; don't go soft, don't explain-apologise or done for; look-tone suggesting tough
(c) Divorce (d) Flee
**Answer:** (b) — Toughness also limits; Fitzgerald begs stop; coaches firmness sustaining respect.

**10.** Ending —
(a) Separation (b) Family game rummy, children cook supper while Mother talks Father; George Suits me, Doris lovely; clusters round Mother
(c) Court (d) Holiday alone
**Answer:** (b) — Apprehension replaces arrogance; attention-thanks-company replace orders-absence; love with dignity.

## Step 2 — 2 School Extracts (RTC 1x4)

> Extract-1: "Put your foot down... be the mistress of your own house and the boss of your own family. They give you orders as if you were the servant... the ruin of them as well as you."

**Q1. Who to whom? (1)**
**Answer:** Mrs Fitzgerald, fortune-teller neighbour, to Mrs Annie Pearson laying cards could be good-bad depends now.

**Q2. Condition described? (1)**
**Answer:** Doris silk ironed, Cyril things-tea, George tea ready yet snooker club; no wages thanks, stays home nightly; unpaid servant taken natural.

**Q3. Why swap needed? (1)**
**Answer:** Pearson too fond hating unpleasantness only drops hints, admits cannot confront; stronger lends bold personality shock; guides restoration affection survives.

**Q4. Chant? (1)**
**Answer:** Arshtatta dum-lam-lamdumbona learnt out East; personalities transpose — bold in Pearson, timid next door.

> Extract-2: "They call you Pompy-ompy Pearson... slow and pompous... leave your wife home night after night. Work eight hours, I work forty... if you shout... I'll slap your big, fat, silly face... rummy... supper."

**Q1. Who mocked and who confirmed? (1)**
**Answer:** Fitzgerald-as-Pearson to George about club snooker mates; son Cyril admits yes Dad afraid they do; staggers hit head.

**Q2. Forty-hour logic? (1)**
**Answer:** To Cyril claiming eight-hour day Union bar: she joined movement, now forty-hour week, two days off weekend; favour only if asked nicely thanked.

**Q3. Slap threat context? (1)**
**Answer:** Warns George on manners after rude shouting; also calls Cyril silly spoilt young piecan for silly old bag insult.

**Q4. Ending formula? (1)**
**Answer:** Don't explain-apologise, keep firm look-tone; rummy together, children supper, Mother-Father talk; Suits me, clusters love.

## Step 3 — 6 Short Answers (40-50 words)

**Q1. Contrast Pearson-Fitzgerald?**
**Answer:** Pearson pleasant worried fortyish flurried, fond hates unpleasantness, drops hints can't say no. Fitzgerald older heavier strong sinister deep smokes, bold decisive from twelve East years. Swap lets timid borrow toughness; tough learns limits begs back — balance.

**Q2. What said to Doris?**
**Answer:** Refused tea Yours isn't, smoking patience, may eat Clarendon. Mocked yellow silk ironing and Charlie Spence buck teeth half-witted — somebody better at her age. Cited twice hours no wages thanks, reducing spoilt daughter nearly tears.

**Q3. What said to Cyril?**
**Answer:** Couldn't bother tea, dislikes mending. Turned Union logic — joined movement Changes. Imposed forty-hour week, two days off weekend, favour bed-cook only if asked nicely thanked. Exposed whispering giggling selfishness demanding rights without duties.

**Q4. How George vanity punctured?**
**Answer:** Laughed stout-hour and unwanted tea — tell club bar. Revealed mates call Pompy-ompy slow pompous, laughing behind back while leaving wife nightly. Cyril confirmed yes Dad afraid, leaving self-important man dazed as if hit head.

**Q5. Why don't explain-apologise crucial?**
**Answer:** Explanation invites debate, apology restores doormat — done for. Firm look-tone mystery sustains respect while affection prevents fear rule. Test rummy-suppert-talk proves humbled Suits me lovely clusters — dignity demanded, love kept.

**Q6. Is drama good medium for message?**
**Answer:** Yes — live villa suburb characters make unpaid-servant injustice visible. Laughter at stout, Pompy-ompy, slap lowers defences, satire enters without sermon. Audience laughs yet recognises self; enacting parts turns reflection into home reform.

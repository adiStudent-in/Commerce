## SESSION 1: Methods of Selling

### 1. What is Sale?
- **Sale = act of selling a product in return for money.** Beginning/extension of relation between customer and vendor.

### 2. Retail Selling Methods (8 - Memorise with 1 line each)
1. **Direct sales** - direct personal demonstration and sale to consumer. Two models: single-level + multilevel marketing.
2. **Proforma sales** - sales quote prepared as **proforma invoice.**
3. **Agency-based** - contractual arrangement where **agent negotiates sale of principal's goods** for commission/fee. Types: sales agents, sales outsourcing, transaction sales, consultative sales, consignment sales, telemarketing/telesales, retail sales.
4. **Travelling salesman** - representative visits shops/businesses to show samples and gain orders. Also called commercial traveller, door-to-door salesman, hawker.
5. **Auction sale** - **public sale,** goods sold to **highest bidder.**
6. **B2B sale** - sales to **other businessman,** not individual customer. Industrial/professional sale.
7. **Electronic-based sales** - **web-based** sale, includes B2B + B2C. Uses **EDI (Electronic Data Interchange).**
8. **Indirect sales** - sale by **third-party partner/affiliate,** not company's own personnel.

### 3. Arrangement of Products in Store
- Products from different manufacturers displayed **systematically in shelves based on space.**
- Display prioritised on **manufacturer's competitiveness** - some buy display window + floor space to promote product.

**Need for arranging (why important):**
- Critical for success. Section-wise division gives **clear view, easy to pick** as customer moves, **improves profit.**

**4 Reasons for Rearrangement:**
1. **Repeat customers** - fresh look, customers come back, more products seen, sales volume increases.
2. **Sales promotions** - rearrangement increases customers.
3. **Brand image** - routine monthly rearranging + changing colour schemes builds fresh style.
4. **Seasonal** - display seasonal products in one area.

### 4. Floor Plans - Procedure for Arranging (4)
1. **Straight floor plan** - products in straight line. **Most common in Indian retail, economical.**
2. **Diagonal floor plan** - more visibility for staff + customers. Needs **small space.**
3. **Angular floor plan** - spacious look, lot of space. Used by **high-value/high-end retailers.** Reduces display area, focuses few popular products.
4. **Geometric floor plan** - racks/fixtures create different style. For **trendy products like cosmetics, watches.**

### 5. Identifying Products Responsible for Sale - 5 Steps for CSA
1. Must have full info about store + products being sold.
2. Follow instructions of store manager/supervisor about stock from manufacturers.
3. Manager allocates sections - vegetable, housekeeping, stationery, clothes, etc.
4. CSA responsible for allotted section, keep full record from receipt till sold.
5. Must have complete product knowledge + skills to sell.

## SESSION 2: Sales Promotional Activities

### 1. Meaning + Objectives
- **Sales promotion = fastest growing area of marketing.** Major component, improves sales + higher profits.
- Firm provides **special offers like discounts.** Offers are direct proposition part of deal.
- Includes all activities which **supplement, coordinate and make effective personal selling + advertising.** Stimulates consumers to buy more.

**5 Objectives (short-term tactic to boost sales):**
1. Stimulate inquiries asking detailed info.
2. Increase product trials expecting trial leads to satisfaction.
3. Encourage repurchase, develop loyalty.
4. Initiate inventory building by retailers for ready supply.
5. Get dealers promotional assistance by coordinating with personal selling.

### 2. Sales Promotion Techniques (2 Heads)

**A. Vendor/Producer Originated (5):**
1. **In-store activities** - cash/kind advantage: price off pack, premiums, small gifts, cooperative promotion, sampling, coupons, buy one get one free, multipacks.
2. **Point-of-sale display material** - materials near products customers often buy: special fixtures like paperback racks, dry battery stand, cold drink racks, display sign, leaflets, demonstration.
3. **Trade incentives** - tradeoffs/cash incentives **directly to retailers,** may not reach customers: cash discounts, special credit terms, goods in lieu of cash, staff incentives.
4. **Direct promotion to customers** - at doorsteps: distribution of coupons + free samples.
5. **Pack design** - most important, retailer accepts package over contents, supports better packs.

**B. Retailer Originated (2):**
1. **Window display** - displaying items for sale to **attract customers into store.**
2. **In-store display** - mass display, multiple product promotion, demonstrations, store loyalty cards, free gifts, POS display, celebrity promotion, joint promotion, staff incentives.

### 3. Ways to Influence Customer Buying (10 steps)
1. Identify prospective customer, start dialogue friendly + humorous.
2. Collect relevant info for sale presentation.
3. Enlighten features + advantages vs competitor.
4. Present/demonstrate with patience in interesting manner.
5. Attend objections immediately, clarify issues.
6. Enlighten warranty period + assure services.
7. Maintain continuous relations, get feedback on satisfaction.
8. Improve skills: patience, communication, reliability, politeness, caring.
9. Do not lose temper with aggressive behaviour.
10. If required use sales promotion techniques.

### 4. Nature of Product - Explicit vs Implicit

**Explicit = common agreement on existence + attributes. 5 attributes:**
1. **Physical configuration** - bundle of physical stuff: shape, size, density, odour, taste, texture, colour, weight.
2. **Associated service** - before-sales (demonstration, credit facility) + after-sales (delivery, installation, spare parts, repair, warranties).
3. **Package and brand name** - differentiate vs competitors, highlight features.
4. **Product size** - **Width = product lines, Depth = items in every line, Consistency = similarity among lines.**
5. **Product life cycle** - **introduction, growth, maturity, decline.** Know stage to make selling strategy.

**Implicit = disagreement, perception differs person to person. 4 points:**
1. **Product symbolism** - symbol by form/size/colour/function, varies by needs/social interaction, status/economy/performance.
2. **Communication media** - consumer interpretation mediated by culture/group/personality, study if info given/hidden, what used for, rate, made by whom, where available.
3. **Product perception** - **physio-psychological process,** how perceived influences marketability, reformulate policies.
4. **Product evaluation** - know satisfaction by **comparing efforts vs rewards.**

## SESSION 3: Responding to Questions and Comments

### 1. Basic Rule
- Sales associate is **only person directly approached** by customer. Training gives idea what to say + flexibility to adapt.

### 2. 7 Situations - What to Say (Very Important for Exam)

1. **When unable to answer:**
- Do not panic, show firmness to make things right.
- Never say **"I don't know".** Say **"It is a valid question, let me find out right thing for you".**
- Place customer need over not knowing, take question to concerned staff/superior.

2. **When item not available:**
- Use **positive language.** Positive: "Product will be available next month, I will place order for you." Negative: "Product unavailable." Positive replaces **"I can't..."**

3. **When transferring call:**
- Transfer to concerned department, customers happy if concerns properly addressed.

4. **Comments on product:**
- Customers give valuable comments, but **holding product vision is responsibility of associate.** Do not say **"We will take a look"** - gives false hope, disappoints later.

5. **Favours that cannot be granted:**
- Most requests reasonable - say Yes. Difficult requests - say No with **positive language politely,** show **possible alternatives** - best way to say No.

6. **When product defective:**
- Show **empathy.** Say: "I am so sorry to disappoint you. There was mistake in manufacturing/shipping. Can I send new one?" Objectives: understand frustration + give immediate solution. Can also offer **full refund** as per product/business.

7. **Closing with customer (close conversation, not sale):**
- Aim customer leaves perfectly happy. 3 checks: care about getting what requires, provide right info, need met rightly.
- End with: **"I am happy to help you Sir/Ma'am", "We are happy to serve you".**

### 3. Dealing with Angry Customers (4 steps)
1. **Apologise sincerely** - "I am sorry" mandatory, personal apology.
2. **Sympathise** - "I understand how upsetting that must have been."
3. **Accept responsibility** - for unhappiness, does not mean at fault.
4. **Prepare to help** - fix by replacement order, best effort even if customer cannot calm.

## SESSION 4: Techniques of Closing a Sale

### 1. Meaning
- **Closing = completing sales transaction, final step of personal selling,** customer signing dotted line, associate gets order.
- Process is **adaptive** - each situation different, adapt to customer + buying stage.

### 2. Factors Influencing Successful Closing (5)
1. **Salesman personality** - sum total influencing prospects favourably. Physical: sound health, neat appearance, voice. Mental: alertness, confidence, enthusiasm, observation. Social: friendly, courtesy, cooperation, well-mannered.
2. **Awareness of company policies** - history, origin, changes, reputation, philosophy, policies to clarify queries + induce purchase.
3. **Product information** - models, size, weight, ingredients, packaging, price, discount, benefits, replacement, after-sales.
4. **Need of customer** - study buying behaviour, likes, dislikes, nature, type to adopt strategy.
5. **Information on competing products** - highlight relative merits, closing becomes easy.

### 3. Techniques of Closing (10 - Memorise with keyword)
1. **Natural close = ask for it close** - appear as natural development, customer should not sense special formula.
2. **Order form close** - questions not direct to purchase, avoid rejection. Ex: "Sir, shall I make invoice/bill?"
3. **Alternative close** - assumes customer will definitely buy, only design/colour/style undecided. Present options, provoke final choice.
4. **Summary close** - summarise benefits: "May I highlight main points so we close deal." Best for **unpredictable customers,** most natural.
5. **Cautionary tale close** - motivate undecided to decide now, tell implication of failing to decide at moment. Do not make up stories.
6. **Final objection close** - customer hesitant objection at end, persuade to communicate objection as it affects decision.
7. **Conditional close** - for **very slow decision makers** with intention but excuse to delay. Assure confidence, will be at service, subtle way.
8. **Lost sales approach** - do not feel lost with objection customers, further conversation with balance converts lost to actual sale.
9. **Assumption close** - from manners/body language/comment assume decision made, give painless decision chance.
10. **Balance sheet close** - assist indecisive prospect to list **arguments for vs against** on paper.

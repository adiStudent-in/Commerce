# Retail Unit 1 — Fundamentals of Retailing (Notes)

> **Retail** = selling goods directly to the final customer (the end-user) for their own use.
> The retail industry is the **2nd largest employer** in India (after agriculture) and the **5th largest industry**.

---

## Session 1 : Basics of Retailing

### 1. Meaning of Retail Business

- A **retailer** is a merchant (or agent/business) whose main job is **selling directly to the ultimate consumer**.
- The retailer sits **between the producer/wholesaler and the consumer** in the marketing channel.

**Distribution Channel (who sells to whom):**

```
Manufacturer → Wholesaler → Retailer → Consumer
```

- **Wholesaler**: Buys goods in **large quantities** from manufacturers and sells in **small quantities** to retailers.
- **Retailer**: Buys from wholesalers and sells directly to the final consumer.

### 2. Meaning & Significance
- Retailers perform tasks: **buying, selling, grading**, and gathering info about customer wants.
- Retailers play a key role because they **stay in direct contact** with the customer.
- Reasons for retail boom: rising income, better living standards, competition, and more new products/services.

🔑 **Key Trend:** Earlier people bought from **small neighbourhood kirana shops**. Now supermarkets, department stores, malls, and **e-retailing (online shopping)** are growing.

---

### 3. Organised vs Unorganised Retailing

- **Unorganised retailing** = Traditional, low-cost shops → kirana, paan shop, handcart vendors, mandi/haats.
  - ❌ Poor infrastructure, lack of funds/technology, few skilled workers, **not registered by law**, no regular accounts.
- **Organised retailing** = **Licensed** retailers registered for sales tax/income tax (corporate chains, malls, supermarkets).
  - ✅ More convenience, choice & control; a comfortable, fast shopping experience.

| Base | Organised Retail | Unorganised Retail |
|------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Concept** | Modern, scientific, many items under one roof | Traditional family stores, poor infra, less tech |
| **Registration** | Compulsory by law | Not compulsory |
| **Items** | Many items & varieties | Few items, limited variety |
| **Size & layout** | Large, attractive layout | Small, unorganised layout |
| **Employees** | Many, specialists | Few, do varied tasks |
| **Capital** | High | Low |
| **Job security** | Regular, government rules | Weak lockdown |
| **Style** | Corporate | Sole trader / partnership |
| **Network** | Many branches in many places | One unit, one locality |
| **Examples** | Supermarkets, malls, hypermarkets, teleshopping, Internet | Cobbler, baniya, kirana, paanwala, mandi vendors |

---

### 4. Types of Retail Establishments

**🗂️ (A) Based on merchandise (products) offered:**

1. **Convenience store** – Small shop near homes, daily need items (milk, bread, eggs), open long hours.
2. **Supermarket** – Self-service store, wide variety of food & household products, kept in aisles.
3. **Hypermarket** – Very big store = department store + grocery supermarket (appliances, clothes, food).
4. **Specialty store** – Sells one specialised category only (electronics, jewellery, books, sports).
5. **Departmental store** – Wide range of goods split into departments; high capital; combines decentralised buying & centralised selling; gives home delivery.
6. **Catalogue showroom** – Customers pick from a catalogue, fill order form, showroom fetches goods from warehouse (hard goods like jewellery, electronics).
7. **Discount store** – Sells products at prices lower than normal.
8. **Shopping mall** – Complex of many shops under one roof with connected walkways.
9. **General store** – Small-town/rural store with varied goods including groceries + household items.
10. **Warehouse store** – Large food/grocery stocked, sale & warehouse (deeper discounts; may charge membership).
11. **Variety store** – Sells a wide range of **inexpensive** household goods.

**🏢 (B) Based on ownership:**

1. **Independent retailer** – Small business owned by one person (baniya, kirana, paanwala).
2. **Corporate retail chain** – One of a group of stores under same ownership & management (Reliance, Bata, Food World).
3. **Franchising** – Franchiser gives a franchisee the right to use its brand & systems (McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Van Heusen).
4. **Consumer cooperative** – Store owned & run by the consumers themselves (Apna Bazaar, Mumbai).

**🖥️ Modern/NOV-Store retailers:**

- **E-retailers** – Online selling (Amazon, Flipkart); payment online or at delivery.
- **Vending machines** – Insert money, pick product, machine dispenses it.
- **Automated retail stores** – 24×7 kiosks, accept cards (Zusaat Shops, RedBox).

---

### 5. Functions of a Retailer

1. **Breaking bulk** – Splits big packages into small quantities for customers.
2. **Providing product info** – Shows how a product solves a customer's need.
3. **Providing after-sales customer service** – Home delivery, gift wrapping, credit facility, repairs.
4. **Creating pleasant shopping experience** – Clean, comfortable, friendly store.
5. **Giving feedback to producers** – Passing on customer needs/complaints.

### 6. Essential Requirements of a Retailer
- Set up shop where customers are attracted.
- Stock only what customers need.
- Sell quality goods at competitive prices.
- Keep up with latest market trends.
- Do window & counter display to boost sales.
- Always be accessible to customers.

### 7. Services Retailers Give to Customers
- Sell in small quantities (as per need) at reasonable prices.
- Meet demand & keep required stock.
- Give buying information, display & demonstrate goods.
- Give replacement/return guidance.
- Offer credit to regular, reliable customers.

## 📌 Session 2 : Sales Associate Services to Customers

### 1. Customer vs Consumer
- **Customer** = one who **buys** the product.
- **Consumer** = one who **uses/consumes** the product.
- *You can be both — e.g., you buy & eat a chocolate.*

**Types of customers:**
- **Loyal** – Few in number, but give most repeat sales & profit. Keep in touch regularly.
- **Discount** – Come often only when discounts are offered or buy low-cost items.
- **Impulsive** – Not planning to buy; buy what looks good at that moment. (Hard to handle.)
- **Need-based** – Buy only what they habitually need; frequent but hard to fill.
- **Wandering** – New to industry; only compare products, rarely buy anything.

### 2. Customer Service — Concept
- Customer service = identifying & fulfilling customer needs in a **friendly, helpful** way.
- Includes help **before, during, and after** the sale.

### 3. The 4 Ps of Good Customer Service
1. **Promptness** – Deliver products on time, no delays & cancellations.
2. **Personalisation** – Remember customer's name & preferences; personal touch.
3. **Professionalism** – Be knowledgeable, competent, confident; no anxiety or frustration.
4. **Politeness** – Put the customer first, be welcoming, respectful, and listen.

> 💡 "Happy customers come back for more and promote the brand."

### 4. Customer Service Functions
- **Customer support** – Answer product questions, give info.
- **Pre-sales** – Inform customers about the product before buying.
- **Marketing** – Reply to mails, telemarketing, surveys, campaigns.
- **Sales** – Physical + e-selling.
- **Technical support** – Data check, address update, problem resolution.
- **Social media service** – Support via Facebook, Twitter, Instagram.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction & Its Importance
- Customer satisfaction = when the **value & service provided meet or exceed** the customer's expectations.

**Values / benefits:**
- Shows repeat purchase & loyalty.
- Distinguishes you from competitors.
- Reduces customers leaking away.
- Increases lifetime value of a customer.
- Reduces negative word-of-mouth.
- Helps retain customers.

**Customer retention strategies:**
- Write blogs to educate customers.
- Send emails for promotions.
- Run customer satisfaction surveys.
- Give personalised experiences.
- Set customer expectations early.
- Use social media to build a community.
- Learn & improve from the best.
- Develop questionnaires & surveys.

## 📌 Session 3 : Skills for Handling Retail Business

### 1. What is a "Skill"?
- Skill = the ability to do a task with a pre-determined result within a given time & effort.
- Skills are **general** (time management, teamwork, leadership, motivation) or **specific** (carpentry, barber work).

### 2. Essentials of Skill Development
- **Take stock** – Know where you stand; assess your assets & abilities.
- **Create a plan** – Set a roadmap to reach your goals.
- **Create an environment for development** – Keep practicing, note mistakes, interact professionally with others, watch your health.

### 3. Skills a Sales Associate needs
1. **Patience** – Stay calm with tough/difficult customers.
2. **Attentiveness** – Pay close attention to details; accurate transactions & displays.
3. **Communication** – Listen well & explain product benefits clearly.
4. **Product knowledge** – Know your products fully to answer correctly.
5. **Positive language** – Use words that create happy customers.
6. **Acting skills** – Handle boring, angry, complaining customers professionally.
7. **Time management** – Solve customer problems within limited time.
8. **Goal oriented** – Focus on tasks & end results.
9. **Resilience** – Stay clear-minded & quick under pressure; don't take things personally.

## 📌 Session 4 : Duties & Responsibilities of a Sales Associate

**Selling where associate** – Manages all sales activities: greeting customers, answering questions, offering help, suggesting items, giving product info.

### 1. Duties of a Sales Associate
- **Handling problems** – Resolve/process customer complaints, ensure they're valid & said within authority.
- **Assisting sales** – Help customers buy; restock & merchantise to boost sales.
- **Clerical tasks** – Receive goods, total bills, accept payments, take orders.
- **Job-specific duties** – Greet customers, answer queries, run cash registers, manage transactions, balance drawers.

### 2. Responsibilities
- **Interact with customers** – In person, phone, email, or chat (even remote).
- **Work directly with customers** in the store.
- **Resolve customer issues** – Wrong price, returns, etc.
- **Listen to customers** – Good listening solves problems fast.
- **Remain friendly and polite.**

### 3. Special Activities of a Sales Associate
- Prepare for & respond to customer inquiries
- Document customer inquiries
- Improve quality of service
- Identify new products & update job knowledge
- Participate in educational/learning opportunities

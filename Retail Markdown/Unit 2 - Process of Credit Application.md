## SESSION 1: Features and Conditions for Credit Sales

### 1. What is Credit Sale?
- **Credit sale =** customer takes product **now**, agrees to **pay later.** It is a type of **trade credit.**
- Creates **receivables** = money owed to company by customers.
- Payment terms: often **within one month** of invoice date, may be longer.
- Collected as: **lump-sum payment, Hire Purchase system, Installment Purchase System.**

**3 Benefits - why retailers sell on credit:**
1. **Meet competition** - if competitors give credit, you must give to stay competitive.
2. **Increase sales** - if competitors don't give credit, you gain sales by giving credit.
3. **Better loyalty** - shows respect and trust, customer keeps buying from you.

### 2. Characteristics of Credit Sale (8)
1. Transferor normally deals in goods and services.
2. Title of goods lies with seller before sold on credit.
3. Fewer formalities, especially in open account.
4. Usually extended for **three months.**
5. Depends on terms imposed by seller.
6. **No security required.**
7. Can be facilitated with financial institutions on easy terms.
8. Almost **half of short financial need** of retail met by mutual trust and good relation.

### 3. Credit Sale Agreement + Retail Credit Facility
- **Credit sale agreement =** agreement for sale of goods where purchase price or part of it is **payable by installments.**
- **Retail credit facility =** financing method giving **loan to retail consumers** to purchase goods/services. For high-value items when short on capital. Enables more consumers to access goods.
- **Risk of default** is main reason for **high interest rate** charged.

**Structure of credit agreement includes:**
loan warranties, lending amounts, interest rates, loan duration, default penalties, repayment terms, basic info of customer + purpose of loan.

**Repayment terms:**
- Term loan: interest rate + date of repayment.
- Revolving loan: minimum payment + recurring payment dates.
- Also mentions if interest can change + maturity date.

### 4. Contract of Sale
- **Contract of sale =** legal contract where seller agrees to **transfer goods to buyer at a price.** Exchange for money paid or promise to pay.
- Made when there is **offer + agreement to buy/sell for price.**
- Can be **written or oral (word of mouth).**
- Generic term includes: **(a) Sale + (b) Agreement to sell.**
- **Agreement to sell =** terms and conditions of sale including amount + future date of full payment.

**5 Essential Elements:**
1. **Essentials of valid contract** as per Indian Contract Act, 1872 - competent parties, free consent, lawful object.
2. **Bilateral contract** - at least **two distinct parties:** seller and buyer.
3. **Transfer of property** - general property transferred from seller to buyer.
4. **Goods** - subject-matter must be some goods.
5. **Price** - consideration is price, may be partly money + partly goods.

### 5. Condition vs Warranty (Very Important)

- **Condition =** fundamental stipulation, must happen before completion of another action.
- **Warranty =** additional stipulation, written assurance by seller to repair/replace if needed.

| Base | Condition | Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Requirement before completion of another action | Assurance about state of product, facts are genuine |
| Defined in | Sec 12(2), Sale of Goods Act, 1930 | Sec 12(3), Sale of Goods Act, 1930 |
| Relation | Directly linked to objective of contract | Subsidiary to object of contract |
| Breach result | Termination of contract | Claim damages only |
| Violation | Violation of condition can become violation of warranty | Violation of warranty does not affect condition |

## SESSION 2: Credit Checks and Getting Authorisation

### 1. Meaning + Need of Credit Check
- **Credit check =** search by retailer to evaluate customer's **creditworthiness** - whether customer can handle money and meet credit requirements.
- Reduces risk of **bad debts.**

**Why needed:**
- Protects interests of both parties, ensures capacity to enter transaction.
- Done whenever customer applies for loan, hire purchase, credit card, store card, line of credit.
- Gives info about mortgage, credit cards, overdrafts, personal loans, car finance, hire purchases, phone repayment history.
- **Credit record =** account of any credit for last **six years.** Shows money accessed + failures.
- Lender takes **consent** to check file - sees address, commitments, reliability. Criteria vary on financial profile + history.

**Positive credit reporting:**
- Earlier only **negative** behaviour (failures) counted.
- Now score also uses other activity like previous enquiries, shows clear picture + recovery from past difficulties.

### 2. Info Collected for Credit Check (4)
1. **Release of information** - need customer's permission.
2. **Signature** - means read, understood and agrees to terms.
3. **Address** - correct address to pull right info.
4. **Employment** - employment history, how long at each job.

### 3. Legal Procedure + Steps Before Granting Credit
- Credit history is **private.** Retailer must take **permission** before accessing legally.
- **How report made:** customer gives info, retailer accesses info and makes report. Can directly ask customer and write.

**7 Steps before granting credit:**
1. **Create credit policy** - payment policies and expectations.
2. **Customer completes application** - key info about customer.
3. **Check references** - ask customers to list references.
4. **Run credit check** - reveals outstanding payments.
5. **Request personal guarantee** - not compulsory for retail store.
6. **Take security interest** - charge security as customer may refuse to pay.
7. **Set credit limits + payment terms** - decide limit for creditworthy customers + days after delivery for full payment.

## SESSION 3: Processing Credit Requisitions

### 1. Requisition vs Credit Requisition vs Credit Limit
- **Requisition =** formal request by buyer to seller to sell desired goods on agreed conditions. Includes brand/model, quantity, delivery date.
- **Credit requisition =** requisition to provide **credit facility** for purchase. Valid requisition includes amount + type of credit, credit score, report, means of security.
- **Credit limit =** maximum amount extended through line of credit, maximum allowed to purchase in store. Normally **prescribed by vendor.**

### 2. Info in Credit Requisition Document (7)
1. Desired items or services.
2. Possible vendors to fulfill order.
3. Budget quotations/proposals - vendor name + details.
4. Delivery instructions.
5. Initial capital details.
6. Contact information.
7. Related accounting details.

### 3. Process of Applications - 5 Performance Criteria
1. Identify customer's needs and provide credit facility.
2. Clearly explain features and conditions to customer.
3. Customer submits application, requisition must be approved by retailer.
4. Promptly refer difficulties in processing.
5. Once approved, requisition goes to **Sales Manager for authorisation.**

## SESSION 4: Techniques for Determining Credit Worthiness

### 1. Meaning
- **Creditworthiness =** valuation by retailer that determines **possibility of customer default** based on earlier debt obligations. Considers **repayment history + credit score.**
- Measured by **Credit Reporting Agencies.**
- When retailer extends credit, it gives loan equal to amount of purchases.

**How to improve score (4):** pay on time, pay more than minimum, pay debt faster, reduce late fees.

### 2. How to Check Worthiness (3)
1. **Require credit application** - every customer must fill it.
2. **Check publicly available information.**
3. **Use credit evaluation tools** to calculate worthiness. Also study income statement + balance sheet.

### 3. Five Cs of Credit (Most Important - Memorise)
1. **Character** - integrity + willingness to repay.
2. **Capacity** - cash inflow + ability to repay debt.
3. **Capital** - financial net worth.
4. **Collateral** - security against credit.
5. **Conditions** - economic, family and personal conditions.

## UNIT 2 - One Page Final Revision

- **Credit sale:** take now, pay later. Trade credit, creates receivables. 1 month term. Lump-sum / Hire Purchase / Installment.
- **Benefits:** meet competition, increase sales, better loyalty.
- **Agreement:** price by installments. **Facility:** loan for retail consumers. Default risk = high interest.
- **Contract of sale:** transfer goods at price, written/oral, Sale + Agreement to sell. 5 essentials: valid contract, bilateral (2 parties), transfer of property, goods, price.
- **Condition 12(2)** - breach = termination. **Warranty 12(3)** - breach = damages.
- **Credit check:** evaluate worthiness, reduce bad debts. Record 6 years. Permission compulsory.
- **7 steps:** policy, application, references, check, guarantee, security interest, limits + terms.
- **Requisition:** formal request. **Limit:** max amount, set by vendor. Approved by Sales Manager.
- **Worthiness:** possibility of default. **Five Cs:** Character-integrity, Capacity-cash flow, Capital-net worth, Collateral-security, Conditions-economic/family.

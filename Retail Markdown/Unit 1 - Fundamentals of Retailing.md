## SESSION 1: Basics of Retailing

### 1. Who is a Retailer?

- **Retailer =** merchant / agent / business enterprise whose main business is **selling directly to ultimate consumers (end-users).**
- Retailer is an **intermediary** in marketing channel - both marketer and customer.
- Retailer performs: **buying, selling, grading, developing information about customer's wants.**
- Retailer maintains **direct contact** with consumer and producer.
- Manufacturers depend on retailers because direct selling to consumers is **inconvenient, expensive and time-consuming.**

### 2. Distribution Channel

**Manufacturer -&gt; Wholesaler -&gt; Retailer -&gt; Consumer**

- **Wholesaler:** buys in **large quantity** from manufacturer, sells in **small quantity** to retailer. Operates **between manufacturer and retailer.**
- **Retailer:** operates **between wholesaler and consumer.** Sells in small quantities to final consumer.

### 3. Organised vs Unorganised Retailing

**Unorganised Retailing:**

- Traditional low-cost formats: **local Kirana shops, owner-manned general stores, paan/beedi shops, convenience stores, hand cart, pavement vendors, Mandis, Haats, Melas, cobbler, fruit-vegetable vendor.**
- Features: poor old infrastructure, insufficient funds, lack of technology, insufficient upstream processes, absence of skilled manpower.
- **Not registered** under any legal provision, does not maintain regular accounts. Small and scattered units.

**Organised Retailing:**

- Trading by **licensed retailers** - registered for sales tax, income tax, etc.
- Includes: **corporate-backed hypermarkets and retail chains + privately owned large retail businesses.**
- Examples: **supermarkets, departmental stores, hypermarkets, shopping malls, multilevel marketing, teleshopping, Internet/e-tailing.**
- Gives more **convenience, choice, control, comfort and speed.** Creates employment.

**Difference Table (Most Important for Exam):**


| Base             | Organised                                                | Unorganised                                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Concept          | Modern, many items under one roof, systematic/scientific | Family-run traditional, poor infra, no technology    |
| Registration     | Compulsory by law                                        | Not compulsory                                       |
| Items            | Large number + varieties                                 | Very few, limited variety                            |
| Size/Layout      | Very large, attractive                                   | Very small, not proper                               |
| Employees        | Large number, varied specialisations                     | Less number, one person does varied functions        |
| Capital          | Very high                                                | Less                                                 |
| Employment terms | Regular, job security, govt. regulated                   | No job security, outside govt. control               |
| Business style   | Corporate style                                          | Sole-trading / partnership                           |
| Network          | Many branches at different places                        | Narrow, single unit in one locality                  |
| Examples         | Supermarket, mall, hypermarket, teleshopping             | Kirana, Baniya, Paanwala, cobbler, Haat/Mandi vendor |


### 4. Types of Retail Establishments

**A. Based on Merchandise Offered (11 types):**

1. **Convenience stores** - small, in residential areas, long hours, limited daily needs (milk, bread, eggs, vegetables).
2. **Supermarkets** - self-service, wide variety of food + household products in aisles.
3. **Hypermarkets** - **department store + grocery supermarket combined**, very large (appliances, clothing, groceries).
4. **Specialty stores** - one category only (furniture, electronics, sports, jewellery).
5. **Departmental stores** - wide range in different **departments**, huge capital, **decentralised buying + centralised selling**, profit/loss on entire stock, restaurants inside, home delivery.
6. **Catalogue showrooms** - see product in printed/online catalogue, fill order form, associate brings from warehouse. For hard goods.
7. **Discount store** - sells below actual price.
8. **Shopping mall** - complex of shops with interconnecting walkways.
9. **General store** - small town/rural, wide variety + groceries + hardware/electrical.
10. **Warehouse store** - food/grocery at deeper discount than supermarket, may charge membership fee.
11. **Variety store** - wide range of inexpensive household goods.

**B. Based on Ownership (4 types):**

1. **Independent retailer** - small, owned by individual, direct contact (Baniya, Kirana, Paanwala).
2. **Corporate retail chain** - group of stores, same business, same ownership/management, different locations (Reliance, Bata, Arrow, Louis Philippe, Food World).
3. **Franchising** - franchiser grants franchisee right to use trademark + business systems (McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Van Heusen).
4. **Consumer cooperatives** - owned and managed by consumers themselves (Apna Bazaar, Mumbai).

**C. Other Modern Retailers:**

1. **E-retailers** - buy over Internet via website/browser (Amazon, Flipkart). Payment online or on delivery.
2. **Vending machines** - insert money, select product, machine dispenses.
3. **Automated retail stores** - self-service kiosks in airports/malls, accept credit cards, open 24x7 (Zoom shops, Red-box).

### 5. Functions of Retailer (5)

1. **Breaking bulk into small quantities** - open large packages, give as per customer need.
2. **Providing product information** - show how product meets customer need = more sales.
3. **Providing customer services** - friendly help, home delivery, gift wrapping, credit facility, after-sales service.
4. **Creating convenient, comfortable, pleasant shopping experience** - right place, right time, warm welcome, soft music, lighting, space, courteous staff.
5. **Providing feedback to producers** - retailer knows customer needs first-hand, shares with producer to improve product.

### 6. Essential Requirements of Retailers (6)

1. Establish shop where customers are attracted.
2. Stock goods needed by customers.
3. Sell quality goods at competitive price.
4. Be up-to-date about latest market trends.
5. Ensure window and counter display to promote sales.
6. Always be accessible to customers.

**Retailer's Services to Customers (6):**

- Sell in little quantities at reasonable prices
- Meet demand, keep required stock
- Give necessary buying information
- Guide on replacement for damages
- Display and demonstrate goods
- Offer credit to regular/reliable customers

## SESSION 2: Sales Associate Services to Customers

### 1. Concept of Customer

- **Customer =** individual or business that **purchases** goods/services produced by business.
- Two segments: (a) **Entrepreneur** who buys for resale, (b) **End-user** who consumes, does not sell.

**Customer vs Consumer:**

- **Customer buys**, **Consumer uses.** You can be both (buy chocolate + eat it).
- Customer judges quality, so retaining + making new customers is key.

### 2. Types of Customers (5)

1. **Loyal customers** - less in number, give more sales/profit, revisit regularly. Keep in touch, invest time.
2. **Discount customers** - frequent but buy only when discount offered or only low-cost products.
3. **Impulsive customers** - no fixed list, buy what looks good at that moment. Challenge to handle, show all useful products.
4. **Need-based customers** - product-specific, habitual need only, frequent but difficult to satisfy.
5. **Wandering customers** - new in industry, only enquire/compare features, least interest in buying.



### 3. Customer Service

- **Meaning:** identifying and fulfilling customer's needs in friendly helpful manner. Assistance **before, during and after sales** - answering queries, solving problems.

**4Ps of Customer Service (Very Important):**

1. **Promptness** - deliver on time, no delay in delivery/cancellation.
2. **Politeness** - customer needs before your own, welcoming, use name, respectful, listen.
3. **Professionalism** - competent, knowledgeable, expert; no anxiety/frustration/fear/weakness.
4. **Personalisation** - remember name, preferences, unique needs. Makes client feel valued, increases loyalty.

**Customer Service Functions (6):**

1. **Customer support** - interact, answer questions, suggest other products.
2. **Pre-sales** - give necessary product information before sale.
3. **Marketing** - mails reply, telemarketing, campaigns, surveys/polls.
4. **Sales** - physical + e-selling.
5. **Technical support** - data verification, address updates, application support, problem resolution.
6. **Social media service** - service via Facebook, Twitter.

### 4. Customer Satisfaction

- **Definition:** when value and service provided through retail experience **meet or exceed customer expectations.**
- Measure of how products/services meet or surpass expectation.

**Importance/Value (6):**

1. Indicates repurchase intention and loyalty
2. Shows point of discrimination (from competitors)
3. Reduces customer retrenchment (loss)
4. Increases lifetime value of customer
5. Reduces negative word of mouth
6. Helps in retaining customers

**Customer Retention Strategies (8):**

- Write blogs to educate customers
- Send emails for special promotions
- Conduct satisfaction surveys
- Offer personalised experience
- Set expectations early
- Use social media to build engaged group
- Learn from the best
- Develop questionnaires and conduct surveys

## SESSION 3: Skills for Handling Retail Business

### 1. Meaning of Skill

- **Skill = ability to do a task with pre-determined results within given time, energy or both.** Ability to do work with expertise.
- Types: **General** (time management, teamwork, leadership, self-motivation) and **Specific/Domain** (carpentry for carpenter).

### 2. Essentials of Skill Development (3)

Skill development increases efficacy, quality of labour, productivity, economic growth. Tool to empower individuals.

1. **Taking stock** - assess where retailer stands, inventory of personal/professional assets.
2. **Creating a plan** - plan for development to reach goals.
3. **Creating environment for development** - 4 strategies:
   - Practice consistent self-directed learning
   - Note problems/mistakes
   - Interact with peers professionally
   - Pay attention to health

### 3. Skills for Sales Associate (9 - Memorise)

1. **Patience** - diffuse tough situations with difficult customers, stay calm.
2. **Attentiveness** - accuracy in POS/billing and display, attention to detail.
3. **Communication skills** - listen effectively, explain product benefits and transaction process clearly, pleasant approachable manner.
4. **Product knowledge** - fully understand products to answer correctly.
5. **Use Positive Language** - language creates perception, minor changes create happy customers.
6. **Acting skills** - deal with boring/angry/complaining customers professionally.
7. **Time management** - solve customer problems in limited time.
8. **Goal oriented** - focus on tasks and end results, set goals and use strategies.
9. **Resilience** - think clearly, decide quickly, not take things personally in busy/understaffed days.

## SESSION 4: Duties and Responsibilities of a Sales Associate

- **Sales associate = customer service associate.** Responsible for all sales activities: greeting, answering, assisting, suggesting, giving product information, keeping floor stocked, display.

### 1. Duties (4)

1. **Handling problems** - complaint handling, check validity, solve within authority, propose solutions.
2. **Assisting sales** - help identify/purchase, selling, restocking, merchandising. Goal: high-class service + maximise sales/revenue.
3. **Clerical tasks** - receive merchandise, total bills, accept payments, take orders.
4. **Job-specific responsibilities** - greet, respond, improve engagement, operate cash registers, manage transactions, balance drawers.

### 2. Responsibilities (5)

1. **Interact with customers** - face-to-face, phone, email, chat (even remotely from home).
2. **Work directly with customers** in retail store.
3. **Resolve issues** - wrong price, returns, etc.
4. **Listen to customers** - effective listening solves problems easily/quickly.
5. **Remain friendly and polite.**

### 3. Special Activities (7)

- Prepare for customer inquiries
- Respond to customer inquiries
- Document customer inquiries
- Improve quality service
- Identify new products
- Update job knowledge
- Participate in educational opportunities

**Extra skills needed:** product knowledge, informing customers, data entry, analysing information, verbal communication, reporting, managing processes, consulting, multitasking.

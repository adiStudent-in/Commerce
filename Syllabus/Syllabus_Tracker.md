## 5. Mathematics (Code 041) — Theory 80

> Topics marked `*Formative only*` are for reinforcement and meaningful learning; assessed formatively only, not in summative assessments. Schools can integrate these with existing chapters.

### Unit-1: Sets and Functions
- [ ] 1. Sets
  - [ ] Sets and their representations; Empty set; Finite and Infinite sets; Equal sets; Subsets; Subsets of a set of real numbers especially intervals (with notations); Universal set
  - [ ] Venn diagrams; Union and Intersection of sets; Difference of sets; Complement of a set; Properties of Complement
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Practical problems on Union and Intersection of two sets
- [ ] 2. Relations & Functions
  - [ ] Ordered pairs; Cartesian product of sets; Number of elements in Cartesian product of two finite sets; Cartesian product of the set of reals with itself (up to R×R×R)
  - [ ] Definition of relation, pictorial diagrams, domain, co-domain and range of a relation
  - [ ] Function as a special type of relation; pictorial representation; domain, co-domain and range
  - [ ] Real valued functions, domain and range: constant, identity, polynomial, rational, modulus, signum, exponential, logarithmic and greatest integer functions, with their graphs
  - [ ] Sum, difference, product and quotients of functions
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Composition of Functions
- [ ] 3. Trigonometric Functions `[MID]`
  - [ ] Positive and negative angles; Measuring angles in radians and degrees; conversion from one measure to another
  - [ ] Definition of trigonometric functions with the help of unit circle; Truth of sin²x + cos²x = 1 for all x; Signs of trigonometric functions
  - [ ] Domain and range of trigonometric functions and their graphs
  - [ ] Expressing sin(x±y) and cos(x±y) in terms of sinx, siny, cosx and cosy + simple applications
  - [ ] Deducing: tan(x±y), cot(x±y), sinα ± sinβ, cosα + cosβ, cosα − cosβ
  - [ ] Identities related to sin2x, cos2x, tan2x, sin3x, cos3x and tan3x
  - [ ] *Formative only:* General solution of sin y = sin a, cos y = cos a and tan y = tan a

### Unit-II: Algebra
- [ ] *Formative only:* Principle of Mathematical Induction — process of proof by induction; natural numbers as least inductive subset of reals; principle + simple applications
- [ ] 1. Complex Numbers and Quadratic Equations `[MID]`
  - [ ] Need for complex numbers, especially √−1, motivated by inability to solve some quadratics
  - [ ] Algebraic properties of complex numbers; Argand plane
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Polar representation; Statement of Fundamental Theorem of Algebra; solution of quadratic equations (with real coefficients) in the complex number system
- [ ] 2. Linear Inequalities `[MID]`
  - [ ] Linear inequalities; Algebraic solutions of linear inequalities in one variable and representation on the number line
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Graphical solution of linear inequalities in two variables; Graphical method of finding solution of system of linear inequalities in two variables
- [ ] 3. Permutations and Combinations `[MID]`
  - [ ] Fundamental principle of counting; Factorial n (n!); Permutations and combinations, derivation of formulae for ⁿPᵣ, ⁿCᵣ and their connections, simple applications
- [ ] **Mid-Term checkpoint: complete above by 5th Sep 2026 + Revision + Mid-Term Exam + Discussion of Mid-Term Question Paper**
- [ ] 4. Binomial Theorem `[Final Term]`
  - [ ] Historical perspective; statement and proof for positive integral indices; Pascal's triangle; simple applications
  - [ ] *Formative only:* General and middle term in binomial expansion
- [ ] 5. Sequence and Series `[Final Term]`
  - [ ] Sequence and Series; Arithmetic Mean (A.M.); Geometric Progression (G.P.), general term, sum of n terms, infinite G.P. and its sum, geometric mean (G.M.), relation between A.M. and G.M.
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Special sums Σk, Σk², Σk³

### Unit-III: Coordinate Geometry `[Final Term]`
- [ ] 1. Straight Lines
  - [ ] Brief recall of 2D geometry; Slope of a line and angle between two lines
  - [ ] Various forms: parallel to axis, point-slope, slope-intercept, two-point, intercept form
  - [ ] Distance of a point from a line
  - [ ] *Formative only:* Normal form; General equation of a line

  - [ ] Sections of a cone: circles, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola; a point, a straight line and a pair of intersecting lines as degenerated case

  - [ ] Limits of polynomials and rational functions, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions
  - [ ] Definition of derivative, relate to slope of tangent; derivative of sum, difference, product and quotient of functions (polynomial and trigonometric)

### Unit-V: Statistics and Probability `[Final Term]`

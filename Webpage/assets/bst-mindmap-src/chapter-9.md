# Chapter 9 — Internal Trade

## Internal trade: meaning and features
- Buying and selling of goods and services within a country's geographical limits
- Payments made and received in the home currency only
- Four features: inside country, home currency, few formalities, local transport
- Memory hook: place, currency, paperwork, transport

## Two types and the chain
- Two types of internal trade: wholesale trade and retail trade
- Chain: manufacturer to wholesaler to retailer to consumer
- Wholesaler is first intermediary; retailer is last link
- Wholesaler buys in bulk; retailer buys from the wholesaler
- Wholesaler sells small lots to retailer; retailer sells to consumer
- Milk example: producer packs 1,00,000 packets; wholesaler takes 10,000

## Wholesale trade: 7 features
- Wholesale trade means trade in which goods are sold in large quantities
- Link between producer and retailer; first intermediary
- Generally one line of product, e.g. stationery, foodgrains
- Large capital invested in maintaining stock of goods
- Sells small lots to retailers, generally not to consumers
- Low margin of profit; buys directly from manufacturer in bulk

## Retail trade: 8 features
- Retail trade means sale of goods in small lots to final consumers
- Retailer buys from wholesaler and sells directly to consumers
- Last link in chain; middleman between wholesaler and consumer
- Deals in variety of products in small quantities
- Residential location; personal relations; contact by phone, Internet, TV, showroom

## Five classification bases
- Size: from small stores to big departmental stores
- Product mix: general stores or single line speciality stores
- Pricing: fixed price or bargaining; market shops costlier
- Service level: assistance, home delivery, credit or self-service
- Ownership: sole proprietorship, partnership, company or cooperative

## Four itinerant retailers
- Itinerants have no fixed place; move from place to place
- Also called mobile vendors; deal in low price, daily use, non-standard items
- Four types: hawkers and pedlars, periodic market traders, cheap jacks, street traders
- Hawker uses wheeled carts or animals; pedlar uses head or back
- Periodic market traders sell on fixed days; Monday Market at Karol Bagh
- Cheap jacks use hired shops or tents; street traders use busy pavements

## Five small-scale shops
- Fixed shop retailers have a fixed place; small-scale or large-scale
- Five small-scale: general store, single line store, street shop, second-hand shop, seconds shop
- General store: small residential shop for daily use items
- Single line store: one product line; also called speciality store
- Street shop: temporary platform at crossing; also called street stall
- Second-hand shop sells used goods; seconds shop sells new defective goods

## Large-scale fixed shops
- Large-scale: large stock, bulk purchase, huge investment, big showrooms
- Large-scale fixed shops also include mail order retailing and automatic vending machines
- Departmental store: large showroom with many departments under one roof
- Each department specialises in one line; central buying, middlemen eliminated
- Departmental: wide range, central location, services, large-scale advertising
- Departmental: Big Bazaar, Reliance; chain: Bata, Pizza Hut
- Chain store: identical shops citywide under one owner; middlemen often eliminated
- Chain: generally one line, central purchase, decentralised sale, branded goods, fixed prices, cash only

## Departmental vs chain: 10 bases
- Variety: departmental wide range; chain one line, limited range
- Credit and pricing: departmental cash or credit, prices vary; chain cash only, fixed uniform
- Location and customers: departmental central, higher income; chain localities, all incomes
- Risk and flexibility: departmental greater risk, more flexible; chain risk spread, little flexibility
- Services and decoration: departmental extra services, varied decor; chain no extra services, uniform
- Advertising: departmental local city; chain national level
- Memory hook: variety, credit, location, risk, customers, services, decoration, pricing, advertising, flexibility

## Wholesaler's services to manufacturers
- Seven services: production, marketing, advice, storage, finance, risk, continuity
- Facilitates large scale production; economies of scale are large-scale benefits
- Helps marketing by persuading retailers to display and sell goods
- Expert advice on customer preferences and competitors' prices
- Storage and risk bearing: theft, spoilage, price fall, fire
- Financial assistance: cash payment or advance; manufacturer invests minimum capital

## Wholesaler's services to retailers
- Seven services: goods, credit, risk, knowledge, publicity, marketing, scale
- Availability of goods from various manufacturers
- Grant of credit reduces working capital needs
- Risk sharing: demand change, transit damage, theft, spoilage
- Specialised knowledge and publicity through pamphlets, banners
- Marketing support; big discount passed on as low margin

## Retailer's services to others
- Seven services to consumers: availability, selection, information, demonstration, delivery, convenience, credit
- Five services to wholesaler and manufacturer: distribution, personal selling, production, promotion, information
- Salesmen explain features; demonstration and after sale service
- Free home delivery; stores in residential areas and markets
- Short-term credit by retailer; long-term through banks and finance companies

## Mail order business
- Selling goods through post or mail without direct contact
- Listed under large-scale fixed shops as mail order retailing
- Seven features: no contact, advertisement and catalogues, mail orders, post delivery, payment, wide market, low cost
- Orders by letter or online; delivery by post; payment by COD, bank transfer or online
- Merits: wide market, low cost; limitation: no face-to-face contact

## GST: meaning and slabs
- Goods and Services Tax, introduced in India from July 2017
- Single national tax on supply of goods and services
- Merged service tax, VAT, excise duty; imports pay IGST plus customs
- Four slabs: 5, 12, 18 and 28 per cent
- Dual GST: CGST, SGST and IGST; destination-based consumption tax
- No tax on exports and supplies to SEZ; removes cascading effect

## GST Council numbers
- Chairperson: Union Finance Minister; vice chairperson from state ministers
- 30 members: MOS Finance and state finance ministers; quorum 50 per cent
- States two-thirds weightage; centre one-third weightage
- Decisions taken by 75 per cent majority
- Neutral body recommending on all GST rules and rates

## Numbers and traps
- Four features, two types, seven wholesale features, eight retail features
- Seven plus seven wholesaler services; seven plus five retailer services
- Five classification bases, four itinerants, five small-scale shops
- Trap: wholesaler links manufacturer and retailer, not customer
- Trap: seconds shop sells new defective goods; chain cash only; GST slabs 5, 12, 18, 28

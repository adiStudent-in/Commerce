# Chapter 4 — Business Services

## Service sector meaning
- Service sector: activities needed for uninterrupted production and exchange of goods
- Specialised services remove the various hindrances of trade
- Twin goals: produce goods in time and reach consumers on time
- Service sector is the backbone of every business and industry

## Five features of services
- 5 features (hook I-I-I-I-C): intangibility, inconsistency, inventory loss, inseparability, participation
- Intangibility: cannot be seen or touched; quality known after experience
- Inconsistency: same service differs by provider and by customer demand
- Inventory loss: produced and consumed together; unconsumed service is total loss
- Inseparability: service cannot be separated from its provider, e.g. doctor
- Customer participation or involvement: customer must take part, e.g. make a call

## Goods vs services: 6 bases
- 6 bases (hook I-I-I-S-N-T): intangible, inventory, inseparable, standardisation, nature, type
- Services intangible and inseparable; goods tangible and separable
- Services need no inventory; goods must maintain inventory
- Services heterogeneous and hard to standardise; goods homogeneous and standardisable
- Service is an activity, e.g. movie; good is an object, e.g. car

## Types of services: 3 and 5
- 3 types: personal, business and social services
- Personal: tourism and restaurants; differs by provider and customer
- Business services help an enterprise carry on business more smoothly
- Social: voluntary, for a social goal; health care, sanitation, education
- 5 business categories: telecom, banking, warehousing, insurance, transportation
- Memory hook T-B-W-I-T fixes the five; social services are a type, not a category

## Bank: meaning and 5 accounts
- Bank attracts deposits to lend to industry or trade; it buys and sells money
- Banking Regulation Act, 1949: accepting public deposits for lending or investment
- 5 functions: accept deposits, pay interest, invest, grant loans, earn margin
- 5 accounts: savings, current, recurring, fixed, multiple option (S-C-R-F-M)
- Savings: small deposit, cheque book, passbook, debit card; withdraw anytime
- Current: for businessmen, cheque withdrawals, overdraft, no interest, service charge
- Recurring: fixed amount every month for a fixed period, repaid with interest
- Fixed: six months to five years, highest interest, fixed deposit receipt
- Multiple option: savings or current plus FD; excess auto-transferred to FD

## Bank services: 5 types
- 5 services: demand draft, banker's cheque, overdraft, cash credit, loans
- Demand draft remits money for a commission; it can never be dishonoured
- Banker's cheque or pay order: local draft, payable within town, lower commission
- Overdraft: current account holder withdraws above balance up to a limit and period
- Cash credit: withdrawal against shares, stocks and bonds for working capital
- Loans: short under 1 year, medium 1-5 years, long over 5 years

## Digital payment and e-banking
- Digital payment: online payment without physical exchange of money
- 6 methods: debit card, credit card, NEFT, RTGS, ATM, e-cash
- Debit card pays up to credit balance; credit card gives overdraft up to a limit
- ATM: 24-hour teller at under half the cost of human tellers
- E-cash: Paytm and PhonePe; needs software and an e-cash bank account
- NEFT: net settlement, direct transfer, no minimum value
- RTGS: RBI-controlled, real time gross, minimum ₹2,00,000, final and irrevocable
- E-banking customers: transparency, 24x7 for 365 days, convenience, discipline, access, cash safety
- E-banking banks: competitive advantage, unlimited network, reduced branch load

## Insurance: meaning and terms
- Insurance: insurer makes good the insured's loss for a regular premium
- Written contract is the insurance policy; 4 terms: insurer, insured, happening, premium
- Insurer pays compensation; insured receives it and pays the premium
- Happening of the event: subject matter or kinds of loss covered

## Seven principles of insurance
- 7 principles: good faith, insurable interest, indemnity, contribution, subrogation, causa proxima, mitigation (G-I-I-C-S-C-M)
- Utmost good faith: both parties disclose all material facts truthfully
- Insurable interest: insured must have financial interest in the subject matter
- Indemnity: restore pre-loss position; not applicable to life insurance
- Contribution: each insurer pays sum assured times loss divided by total sum insured
- Subrogation: after compensation, insurer steps into the insured's shoes
- Causa proxima: the loss must be caused by an insured risk
- Mitigation: insured must take reasonable care to save the property

## Four types and comparisons
- 4 types: life, fire, marine, health; health pays medical expenses of insured and family
- Life: sum assured paid at death or maturity, whichever is earlier
- Fire: indemnity for fire damage; policy period generally one year
- Marine: sea perils; cargo, hull and freight policies
- Health covers basic, major, long-term hospitalisation, medical supplement, disability income
- Insurable interest: life at policy, fire at policy and loss, marine at loss
- Life has surrender value and covers investment; fire and marine do not
- Life 5-30 years or whole life; fire 1 year; marine 1 year or voyage
- Trap: life is assurance, not general insurance; general covers fire, marine, health

## Postal services
- Communication: exchange of ideas and messages; 2 types are postal and telecom
- Postal: government-controlled, national and international, highly reliable
- Postal drawbacks: slow speed and bureaucratic nature; businessmen prefer private couriers
- Courier gives desk-to-desk service, picking up and delivering at the office
- Mail services: UPC, registered post, parcel post and speed post
- Postal financial facilities: Provident Fund, Kisan Vikas Patra, NSC, monthly income schemes, savings account

## Six telecom services
- 6 telecom services: cellular mobile, radio paging, fixed line, cable, VSAT, DTH
- Cellular mobile: voice, non-voice, messages, data and PCO services
- Radio paging: affordable tone, numeric, alpha; cable: voice, data, entertainment
- Fixed line: fibre optic cables across the country; links any telecom provider
- VSAT: satellite-based; serves remote areas with market rates and tele-education
- DTH: satellite media needing a small dish antenna and a set-top box

## Warehousing: 4 types
- Warehousing means storing goods safely until they are needed or shipped out
- 4 types: private, public, bonded and government warehouses
- Private: owned and used by one business; gives exclusive control
- Public: available for anyone to use, on rent
- Bonded: imported goods stored without customs duty until cleared
- Government: run by government agencies for public use
- Trap: exclusive control points to a private warehouse, not a public one

## Exam numbers and traps
- RTGS minimum ₹2,00,000; NEFT has no minimum value
- Interest order: current nil, savings, recurring, fixed highest
- Trap: demand draft never bounces; banker's cheque is local, lower commission
- Trap: rat spoiling wheat means no claim; rat hole letting sea water in means claim
- Trap: printed key says Inventory is not a characteristic; notes list inventory loss

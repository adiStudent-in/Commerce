CBSE paper design (2026-27) — typology of questions (theory):

  • Accountancy / Business Studies / Economics: Remembering & Understanding 32 (40%) · Applying 24 (30%) · Analysing, Evaluating & Creating 24 (30%)
  • English Core: Reading 26 = Passage 10 + Case-based 8 + Note-making & Summary 8; Grammar 7 + Writing 16 = Advt 3 + Poster 3 + Speech 5 + Debate 5; Literature 31 = Extracts 3+3+4 + Short answers 6+3 + Long answers 6+6
  • Mathematics: Remembering & Understanding 44 (55%) · Applying 20 (25%) · Analysing/Evaluating/Creating 16 (20%); Internal 20 = Tests 10 + Activities 10; no chapter-wise weightage

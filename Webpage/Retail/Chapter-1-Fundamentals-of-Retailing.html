Fundamentals of Retailing

Class 11 Retail (Sales Associate) — Quick Revision Notes

📌 Big Picture

Retail = selling goods directly to the final customer (end-user) for their own use.
Retail industry is the 2nd largest employer in India (after agriculture) and the 5th largest industry.

1. 🛒 Basics of Retailing

1.1 Meaning & Significance of Retail Business

A retailer is a merchant (or agent / business enterprise) whose main business is selling directly to the ultimate consumer.
  • The retailer sits between the producer/wholesaler and the consumer in the marketing channel.
  • Retailers perform many tasks: buying, selling, grading, and gathering information about customer wants.
  • They stay in direct contact with the customer — that is their key strength.
  • Reasons for the retail boom: rising income, better living standards, more new products & services, and growing competition.

Distribution Channel (who sells to whom)

Manufacturer → Wholesaler → Retailer → Consumer
IntermediaryWhat they do
WholesalerBuys goods in large quantities from manufacturers; sells in small quantities to retailers
RetailerBuys from wholesalers and sells directly to the final consumer
💡 Key Trend: Earlier people bought from small neighbourhood kirana shops. Today supermarkets, departmental stores, malls, and e-retailing (online shopping) are growing fast.

1.2 Organised vs Unorganised Retailing

  • Unorganised retailing = Traditional, low-cost shops → kirana, paan shop, handcart vendors, mandi/haats.
    • Poor infrastructure, lack of funds/technology, few skilled workers, not registered by law, no regular accounts.
  • Organised retailing = Licensed retailers registered for sales tax/income tax (corporate chains, malls, supermarkets).
    • More convenience, choice & control; a comfortable, fast shopping experience.

💻 Modern / Non-store retailers

  • E-retailers – online selling (Amazon, Flipkart); payment online or at delivery.
  • Vending machines – insert money, pick a product, machine dispenses it.
  • Automated retail stores – 24×7 kiosks that accept cards (Zoom Shops, RedBox).

1.4 Functions of a Retailer (5)

  1. Breaking bulk into small quantities – splits large packages into small quantities for customers.
  2. Providing product information – shows how a product solves a customer's need.
  3. Providing customer services – home delivery, gift wrapping, credit facility, after-sales service.
  4. Creating a convenient, pleasant shopping experience – friendly staff, good lighting, easy movement, soft music.
  5. Providing feedback to producers – passing on customer needs and preferences.

1.5 Essential Requirements of Retailers

  • Establish the shop where customers are attracted.
  • Stock the goods which are needed by the customers.
  • Sell quality goods at competitive prices.
  • Be up-to-date about the latest market trends.
  • Ensure window and counter display to promote sales.
  • Always be accessible to customers.

Services retailers give to customers

  • Selling goods in small quantities at reasonable prices.
  • Meeting consumer demand and keeping required stock.
  • Providing necessary buying information.
  • Guiding on replacement conditions for damaged goods.
  • Displaying and demonstrating goods to attract customers.
  • Offering credit facility to regular, reliable customers.

2. 🎭 Sales Associate Services to Customers

2.1 Customer vs Consumer

A customer is one who buys the product; a consumer is one who uses the product.
You can be both — e.g., you buy and eat a chocolate.

Types of customers

TypeBehaviour
LoyalFew in number, but give most repeat sales & profit; keep in regular touch
DiscountCome often only when discounts are offered, or buy low-cost items
ImpulsiveNot planning to buy; buy what looks good at that moment (hard to handle)
Need-basedBuy only what they habitually need; frequent but hard to satisfy
WanderingNew to the industry; only compare products, rarely buy

2.2 The 4 Ps of Customer Service

PMeaning
PromptnessDeliver products on time; no delays in delivery or cancellation
PolitenessPut the customer first; be welcoming, respectful, and listen
ProfessionalismBe knowledgeable, competent, confident; avoid anxiety, frustration, weakness
PersonalisationRemember the customer's name, preferences, and unique needs
💡 Customer service functions: Customer support (answering queries) · Pre-sales (info about products) · Marketing (mails, telemarketing, surveys) · Sales (physical + e-selling) · Technical support (data verification, problem resolution) · Social media service (Facebook, Twitter).

2.3 Customer Satisfaction & Its Importance

Customer satisfaction = when the value and service provided meet or exceed the customer's expectations.

Values / benefits of customer satisfaction

  • Indicates repeat purchase & loyalty.
  • Shows a point of discrimination from competitors.
  • Reduces customer loss (retrenchment).
  • Increases the lifetime value of a customer.
  • Reduces negative word-of-mouth.
  • Helps in retaining customers.

Customer retention strategies

  • Write blogs to educate customers.
  • Send emails for special promotions.
  • Conduct customer satisfaction surveys.
  • Offer a personalised experience.
  • Set customer expectations early.
  • Use social media to build an engaged customer community.
  • Learn and improve from the best.
  • Develop questionnaires and conduct surveys.

3. 💡 Skills for Handling Retail Business

Skill = the ability to do a task with a pre-determined result within a given time, energy, or both.

Essentials of skill development

  • Take stock – know where you stand; assess your assets and abilities.
  • Create a plan – set a roadmap to reach your goals.
  • Create an environment for development – practice consistently, note mistakes, interact professionally, watch your health.

4. 📋 Duties & Responsibilities of a Sales Associate

A sales associate is responsible for all sales activities — from greeting customers to answering questions, offering assistance, suggesting items, and providing product information.

Duties

  • Handling problems – resolve/process customer complaints within the bounds of authority.
  • Assisting sales – help customers buy, restock and merchandise to boost sales.
  • Clerical tasks – receive goods, total bills, accept payments, take orders.
  • Job-specific responsibilities – greet customers, answer queries, run cash registers, manage transactions, balance drawers.

Responsibilities

  • Interact with customers (in person, phone, email, or chat — even remotely).
  • Work directly with customers in the store.
  • Resolve customer issues (wrong price, returns, etc.).
  • Listen to customers — good listening solves problems fast.
  • Remain friendly and polite.

Special activities

  • Prepare for and respond to customer inquiries; document them.
  • Improve quality of service.
  • Identify new products and update job knowledge.
  • Participate in educational opportunities.

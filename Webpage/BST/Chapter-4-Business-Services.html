Chapter 4

Business Services

Class 11 Business Studies — Quick Revision Notes

📌 Big Picture

Business Services = services used by business enterprises to conduct their daily activities (banking, insurance, transport, warehousing, communication).

This chapter covers three major business services:

  1. Banking — accepting deposits & lending money
  2. Insurance — protection against financial loss
  3. Postal & Telecom Services — communication & parcel delivery

1. 🧠 Nature of Services (5 I's)

Services are intangible economic activities that provide satisfaction without transferring ownership.

#CharacteristicMeaningExample
iIntangibilityCannot be seen/touched/smelled; only can be feltDoctor's treatment, airline travel
iiInconsistencyQuality varies from provider to provider & customer to customerExperience at different salons
iiiInseparabilityProduction & consumption happen simultaneouslyATM needs customer to be present
ivInventory (Perishability)Cannot be stored for future useEmpty hotel room = lost revenue forever
vInvolvementCustomer participation is essentialCustomer must specify needs for customisation

3. 🏦 Banking Services

3.1 Definition of Bank

Bank = an institution that deals in money and credit. It accepts deposits from the public and lends money for profit.

Banking (as per Banking Regulations Act, 1949):

"Accepting for the purpose of lending or investment of deposits of money from the public, repayable on demand or otherwise, and may be withdrawn by cheque, draft or otherwise."

4. 🛡️ Insurance Services

4.1 Definition

Insurance = a contract in writing (policy) whereby one party (insurer) agrees, in consideration of premium, to indemnify the other party (insured) against loss/damage from a specified event.

Functions of Insurance

FunctionExplanation
Risk SharingSpreads risk among many people exposed to similar perils
Risk EvaluationAssesses risk before accepting & determines premium
Capital FormationPremiums collected are invested in productive assets
Economic StabilityProvides compensation, preventing financial collapse
Providing CertaintyConverts uncertain loss into certain (small) premium payment

4.2 Principles of Insurance (7 Principles)

#PrincipleExplanation
iUtmost Good Faith (Uberrimae Fidei)Both parties must disclose all material facts truthfully
iiInsurable InterestInsured must have financial interest in the subject matter (existing at time of contract for life insurance; at time of loss for others)
iiiIndemnityCompensation cannot exceed actual loss (not applicable to life insurance)
ivProximate CauseNearest / immediate cause of loss determines claim liability
vSubrogationAfter paying claim, insurer gets insured's rights over damaged property (not for life insurance)
viContributionIf multiple insurers, each contributes proportionally; insured cannot profit
viiMitigationInsured must take reasonable steps to minimise loss
💡 Life Insurance — NOT a contract of indemnity (human life cannot be indemnified). Insurable interest must exist only at the time of taking the policy.

4.3 Types of Insurance

TypeSub-typesKey Points
Life InsuranceWhole Life, Endowment, Term, ULIP, Money-backCovers human life; sum assured paid on death/maturity; savings + protection
Fire InsuranceStandard Fire, ComprehensiveCovers loss/damage by fire; indemnity contract; insurable interest at time of loss
Marine InsuranceHull, Cargo, Freight, LiabilityCovers ship (hull), goods (cargo), freight charges; indemnity contract
Health InsuranceMediclaim, Critical Illness, Top-upCovers medical/hospitalisation expenses
Motor InsuranceThird-party, ComprehensiveRequired by law (Motor Vehicles Act); TP covers third-party liability
Burglary/Theft—Covers loss from theft, housebreaking

7. 🏪 Warehousing Services

Warehousing = storage of goods from production until consumption. Removes hindrance of time.

Types of Warehouses:

TypePurpose
PrivateOwned by producers/traders for own goods
PublicOperated by government/agencies; available for rent; licence required
BondedStores imported goods until customs duty paid
Cold StoragePerishable items (fruits, vegetables, meat, dairy)
GodownsSmall-scale rural storage (agricultural produce)

Functions of Warehousing:

  • Scientific storage & preservation
  • Protection from theft, damage, weather
  • Regular supply throughout the year
  • Inventory management & financing

8. 📡 Communication Services & Business

Communication = transfer of information, ideas, instructions. Removes hindrance of information.

For business, communication services enable:

  • Marketing & advertising
  • Customer support & feedback
  • Coordination with suppliers & distributors
  • Digital payments & e-commerce
  • Remote work & video conferencing

1. Introduction

The service sector includes all economic activities required for uninterrupted production and exchange of goods. It provides specialised services that remove various hindrances of trade.

IntangibilityCannot be seen or touched. Quality only known after experiencing it.You can't see/touch a doctor's treatment — you only feel better after it.

5. Banking Services

Bank: An institution that attracts money as deposits from the public and lends it to industry/trade. Acts as a buyer and seller of money, earning profit from the margin between interest charged and interest paid.

Legal Definition (Indian Banking Regulation Act, 1949): "Accepting deposits of money from the public for the purpose of lending or investments."

6. Insurance Services

Insurance: A contract between the Insurer (Insurance Company) and the Insured (Person/Firm). The insured pays regular Premium. The written agreement is the Insurance Policy.
TermMeaning
InsurerThe insurance company that compensates the loss
InsuredThe person/firm who gets compensation
PremiumRegular payment made by insured (monthly/quarterly/yearly)
PolicyThe written contract with terms & conditions
Happening of EventThe specific loss covered under the policy

6.1 Principles of Insurance (7 Principles)

            graph TD
              PI["7 Principles
of Insurance"] --> P1["1. Utmost Good Faith"]
              PI --> P2["2. Insurable Interest"]
              PI --> P3["3. Indemnity"]
              PI --> P4["4. Contribution"]
              PI --> P5["5. Subrogation"]
              PI --> P6["6. Causa Proxima"]
              PI --> P7["7. Mitigation of Loss"]
#PrincipleExplanationExample
1Utmost Good FaithBoth parties must disclose ALL material facts truthfully. No hiding!If you hide a heart disease while taking life insurance, company can refuse claim.
2Insurable InterestInsured must have financial interest in the subject matter.You can insure your spouse/children but NOT a stranger.
3IndemnityInsurance is NOT for profit. Compensation = actual loss (whichever is less).Insured for —2 lakh, loss = —1 lakh → get —1 lakh only.
4ContributionIf multiple insurers, each contributes proportionally. Insured cannot claim full from each.A pays (Sum A / Total) × Loss, B pays (Sum B / Total) × Loss
5SubrogationAfter compensation, insurer gets ownership of damaged property.Half-burnt goods sold for —10,000 → money goes to insurer, not insured.
6Causa ProximaThe nearest/direct cause of loss must be the insured risk.Rat spoiling wheat = no claim. Rat making hole + sea water spoiling = claim!
7Mitigation of LossInsured must take reasonable care to protect the property.Can't just watch your house burn — must try to stop the fire!
🧠 Mnemonic: "GU ICC SM"
Good Faith • Insurable Interest • Contribution • Causa Proxima • Subrogation • Mitigation

Applicability of Principles to Life Insurance:

A. Life Insurance

Covers two risks: (i) Dying too early → family gets support (ii) Living too long → old age financial support. Sum assured paid at death or maturity — whichever comes first. Also called Contract of Assurance.

B. Fire Insurance

Covers financial loss due to fire during a specific period. Usually 1 year (renewed annually). Partial loss also covered. Follows Principle of Indemnity strictly.

Marine Insurance Types:

  • Cargo Insurance: Goods in the ship + belongings of crew/passengers
  • Hull Insurance: The ship itself + equipment (machinery, fuel, engine)
  • Freight Insurance: Loss of freight charges if ship is lost

C. Health Insurance

Covers: Basic Medical Expenses (hospitalisation + doctor), Major Medical Expenses (catastrophic illness), Long-term Hospitalisation (elderly nursing), Disability Income (income replacement).

7.1 Postal Services

Features: Government controlled, national & international service, also offers financial services (PPF, Kisan Vikas Patra, NSC), Speed Post started, highly reliable.

Drawbacks:

  • ❌ Slow in speed compared to private couriers
  • ❌ Bureaucratic — lots of official procedures
  • 💡 Solution: Private courier services — faster, Desk-to-Desk service

Services Offered by Post Office:

ServiceDescription
Mail ServicesSending letters & parcels from one place to another
Financial ServicesSavings schemes (PPF, KVP, NSC, MIS, Savings Account)
Registered PostSecure delivery — receiver signs, sender gets receipt
Parcel PostMost economical way to send parcels across India & abroad
Speed PostTime-bound fast delivery; higher charges than ordinary mail
Courier ServicesPrivate, faster, Desk-to-Desk service
OtherInternational money transfer, e-bill post, etc.

Case Study: RTGS

Mr. Modi wants to transfer —50,000 immediately. Bank manager says minimum for immediate transfer is —2,00,000.

Concept: RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)

Features: Controlled by RBI, real-time settlement, minimum —2,00,000, final & irrevocable, no physical money exchange.

Case Study: Insurance Principles

Case A: Priya insures her boutique AND her friend's boutique. Fire damages friend's boutique. Company rejects claim.

Violated: Principle of Insurable Interest

Case B: Harish takes fire insurance for his factory but doesn't disclose explosive chemicals. Fire damages factory. Company refuses.

Violated: Principle of Utmost Good Faith

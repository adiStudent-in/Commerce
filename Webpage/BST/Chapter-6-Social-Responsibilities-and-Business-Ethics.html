Social Responsibilities of Business and Business Ethics

Class 11 Business Studies — Simple sentences. One idea per line. Read, check, repeat.

1. What is Social Responsibility

Definition: Social Responsibility is the obligation of business towards society.
  • Business cannot survive with only economic objectives.
  • Business must also have social objectives.
  • Social Responsibility is voluntary effort for social well-being.
  • Business cannot exist in isolation.
  • Business uses resources of society.
  • Business earns from members of society.
  • So business must do something for society.
CHECK: Say the definition of Social Responsibility in one sentence.

2. Case for Social Responsibility

Definition: Case for Social Responsibility is reasons why business must do social duty.

ReasonSimple Meaning
Self-interestHelp society now. Get benefit later.
Better EnvironmentGood society gives good workers and customers.
Public ImageHappy people give good name.
Avoid Government ActionDo duty now. Avoid strict law later.
Social PowerMore power needs more duty.
Social ProblemsYou created pollution. You must solve it.

2.1 Self-interest

  • Business is the creation of society.
  • Business uses resources of society.
  • If business does not satisfy society, society can take back resources.
  • A firm that fulfils social obligations gets benefit in the long run.

2.2 Better Environment for Business

  • Social Responsibility improves quality of life.
  • It raises standard of living.
  • Business gets better community for work.
  • Labour becomes better quality. Customers become better quality.
  • So business gets better opportunities.

2.3 Public Image

  • Satisfied employees and customers help business success.
  • Consumers expect better quality at reasonable price. Workers expect fair wages.
  • If business satisfies these expectations, image improves.

2.4 Avoidance of Government Interference

  • Government action is costly for business. It restricts freedom of decisions.
  • Example is Consumer Protection Act.
  • This Act prevents adulteration and black marketing.
  • Social duty avoids government action.

2.5 Social Power

  • Business has considerable social power. It must have matching responsibility.
  • Power and responsibility must stay in balance.
  • Imbalance opens door to irresponsible behaviour.
  • If business avoids duty, government can withdraw some powers.

2.6 Contribution to Social Problems

  • Business has created some social problems. Examples are pollution and unsafe workplaces.
  • Example is discrimination.
  • Business must solve these problems because business operations created them.
CHECK: Business created pollution. Who must solve it? Answer: Business.

3. Responsibility Towards Interest Groups

3.1 Towards Consumers

  • Produce safe items. Maintain quality standards.
  • Ensure regular supply of goods and services.
  • Educate customer about use and contents of product.
  • Be truthful in advertising. Follow fair trade practices.
  • Handle complaints and grievances quickly.
  • Design product as per needs of customers.

3.2 Towards Employees

  • Give fair compensation and benefits.
  • Give good and safe working conditions.
  • Give opportunities for personal growth and development.
  • Develop a sense of belongingness.
  • Give housing, medical, insurance and retirement benefits.

3.3 Towards Owners and Shareholders

  • Ensure safety of investment.
  • Ensure fair and regular return on investment.
  • Give complete information about financial position.
  • Ensure appreciation of investment by proper use of resources.
  • Give opportunities to participate in decision-making.

3.4 Towards Government

  • Abide by rules, regulations and laws. Pay taxes and duties on time.
  • Help in solving social problems.
  • Adopt fair dealing in external trade. Maintain reputation of country.
  • Cooperate in planning, investigation and administrative activities.

3.5 Towards Community

  • Protect environment from all types of pollution.
  • Provide more employment opportunities. Promote national integration.
  • Improve local area near the plant. Provide drinking water, electricity and dispensaries.
  • Help weaker section of society. Provide economic stability.
  • Preserve social and cultural values.

3.6 Towards Suppliers

  • Ensure regular payment to suppliers. Adopt fair dealings.
  • Protect small-scale suppliers. Place orders with small suppliers.
  • Guide suppliers to improve quality.
Note: Interests of all groups are not always in harmony. Businessman has skill to reconcile conflicting interests and serve all groups to their satisfaction.
CHECK: Fair and regular return is duty to whom? Answer: Shareholders. Eco-friendly production is duty to whom? Answer: Community.

4. Business and Environment Protection

  • Greatest problem from industries is pollution.
  • Absence of effective legal control encourages polluting industries in urban areas.
  • Emissions affect health of workers and local residents.
  • Damage increases disease and disaster.

4.1 Causes of Environmental Pollution

Definition: Pollution is injection of harmful substances into the environment.
TypeMeaningMain Cause
Air PollutionHarmful substances in air. Lowers quality of air we breathe. Created hole in ozone layer.Smoke and chemicals from factories. Smoke from vehicles.
Water PollutionDumping of chemical waste in rivers and lakes.Factories near rivers dump waste into water.
Land PollutionDumping of toxic waste on land. Reduces fertility. Makes land unfit for agriculture.Two tasks: improve damaged land and check further contamination.

4.2 Need for Pollution Control

  • Ensure Safety: Smoke reduces visibility. Poor visibility increases highway accidents. It is hazardous for landing and taking off of aeroplanes.
  • Economic Losses: Air pollution blackens buildings. Water pollution affects sea life. Land pollution affects fertility. Example is Taj Mahal loses beauty due to industries around it.
  • Maintain Natural Beauty: Beauty is mountains, rivers, trees and land. Polluted rivers spread foul odour. Buildings get blackened.
  • Ensure Healthy Life: Air pollution causes heart and lung diseases. Water pollution causes liver and kidney problems.
  • Lead a Comfortable Life: Polluted rivers become unfit for swimming or fishing. Air pollution causes eye irritation, cough and breathing problems.

4.3 Role of Business in Environment Protection

  • Use eco-friendly techniques of production. Recycle industrial waste.
  • Treat waste with technology before discharge into water or land.
  • Participate in plantation of trees. Participate in cleaning rivers and ponds.
  • Use eco marks by producing eco-friendly products.
  • Abide by bye-laws and policies of government.
  • Spread awareness about eco-friendly techniques and products.
CHECK: Name two actions. Answer: Recycle waste and plant trees.

5. Business Ethics

Definition: Business Ethics decides expected conduct of businessman and not expected conduct.
  • Ethical behaviour is above behaviour required by law.
  • Both law and ethics decide what is right and what is wrong.
  • Laws reflect ethical values of society. Laws show what conduct is permitted.
  • If one breaks law, one is punished or penalised.
  • Social Responsibility is broader than legal responsibility.

5.1 Elements of Business Ethics

ElementSimple Meaning
Top CommitmentTop managers show ethical example. CEO must be openly committed. Guide middle and low level.
CodeWrite rules in a document. Covers health, safety, fair selling and marketing.
ComplianceCheck that rules are followed. Ethics training in recruitment. Staff can report unethical behaviour.
Involve EmployeesAsk staff before making rules. Form small groups. Discuss policies.
Measure ResultsAudit work as per standards. Top level meets staff to set future plan.
CHECK: Code is what? Answer: Written document of ethics.

6. Practice Questions

Solved Questions From Source

Q1. When firms use eco-friendly method, duty to which group? A: Community.

Q2. Fair and regular return is duty to which group? A: Shareholders.

Q3. Firm fulfils all social obligations and gets long-run benefit. Which reason? A: Self-interest.

Q4. Social Responsibility is? A: Broader than legal responsibility.

Q5. Which concept is about right and wrong? A: Business Ethics.

Case: Inn Trin Ltd (Telecom)

  • Employees: Committed to treat everyone fairly with respect and dignity.
  • Government: Abides by all laws and pays taxes on time.
  • Consumers: Fair advertisement, fair charge, maintain quality.
  • Shareholders: Minimise waste to maximize return.
  • Community: Runs charitable hospital for local people.

Case: Build Well Ltd (Real Estate)

Values are Truthfulness, Respect, Justice, Commitment, Trust, Citizenship. These values are Business Ethics. Elements are Top Commitment, Code, Compliance, Involve Employees, Measure Results.

Exam Questions With Answers

  • Define Social Responsibility. A: Obligation of business towards society.
  • Define pollution. A: Injection of harmful substances into environment.
  • Name three types of pollution. A: Air, Water and Land.
  • Give example of economic loss. A: Taj Mahal loses beauty due to industries.
  • What is Code? A: Written document of ethics for all employees.
  • What is Compliance Mechanism? A: System to ensure decisions match ethical standards.

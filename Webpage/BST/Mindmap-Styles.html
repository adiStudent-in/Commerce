Style 1

Glance Board — the whole chapter on the wall

Nothing hidden anywhere: one card per branch, and under every topic its key facts printed as chips — the exam-ready short version, not a teaser. Read top to bottom once and you have covered the chapter; print it and it becomes your poster.

All facts visible — zero clicking needed Click topic / branch title → open section Print-friendly poster layout
Style 2

Expanding Trunk — open the chapter fold by fold

Chapter bars, then topic rows — each one unfolds exactly its key-fact chips in place. Starts compact for a spine-check; press Expand everything and it becomes the full glance-board; press it again and it's back to a tidy spine. Facts still live here, not in a tooltip.

Click bar → unfold branch Click row caret → unfold topic facts Expand everything → full-glance mode Topic name → open section
Style 3

Fact Sheet Table — one row per topic

A compact chapter cheat-sheet: branch column, topic with its fact chips, and the section jump at the end. The fastest way to self-quiz — cover the middle column, say the facts, check yourself.

Filter pills → one branch only Hover a row → highlight § column → open section

## 1. What is Social Responsibility

**Definition:** Social Responsibility is the obligation of business towards society.

Points:

1. Business cannot survive with only economic objectives.
2. Business must also have social objectives.
3. Social Responsibility is voluntary effort for social well-being.
4. Business cannot exist in isolation.
5. Business uses resources of society.
6. Business earns from members of society.
7. So business must do something for society.

CHECK: Say the definition of Social Responsibility in one sentence.

---

## 2. Case for Social Responsibility

**Definition:** Case for Social Responsibility is reasons why business must do social duty.

### 2.1 Self-interest

1. Business is the creation of society.
2. Business uses resources of society.
3. If business does not satisfy society, society can take back resources.
4. A firm that fulfils social obligations gets benefit in the long run.

CHECK: Who gives resources to business? Answer: Society.

### 2.2 Better Environment for Business

1. Social Responsibility improves quality of life.
2. It raises standard of living.
3. Business gets better community for work.
4. Labour becomes better quality.
5. Customers become better quality.
6. So business gets better opportunities.

CHECK: Social duty gives what to business? Answer: Better environment.

### 2.3 Public Image

1. Satisfied employees and customers help business success.
2. Consumers expect better quality at reasonable price.
3. Workers expect fair wages.
4. If business satisfies these expectations, image improves.

CHECK: Good social work improves what? Answer: Public image.

### 2.4 Avoidance of Government Interference

1. Government action is costly for business.
2. It restricts freedom of business decisions.
3. Example is Consumer Protection Act.
4. This Act prevents adulteration and black marketing.
5. Social duty avoids government action.

CHECK: Name one Act. Answer: Consumer Protection Act.

### 2.5 Social Power

1. Business has considerable social power.
2. It must have matching responsibility.
3. Power and responsibility must stay in balance.
4. Imbalance opens door to irresponsible behaviour.
5. If business avoids duty, government can withdraw some powers.

CHECK: What must balance power? Answer: Responsibility.

### 2.6 Contribution to Social Problems

1. Business has created some social problems.
2. Examples are pollution and unsafe workplaces.
3. Example is discrimination.
4. Business must solve these problems.
5. Reason is business operations created them.

| Reason | Simple Meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| Self-interest | Help society now. Get benefit later |
| Better Environment | Good society gives good workers and customers |
| Public Image | Happy people give good name |
| Avoid Government Action | Do duty now. Avoid strict law later |
| Social Power | More power needs more duty |
| Social Problems | You created pollution. You must solve it |

CHECK: Business created pollution. Who must solve it? Answer: Business.

3. Ensure regular supply of goods and services.
4. Educate customer about use and contents of product.
5. Be truthful in advertising.
6. Follow fair trade practices.
7. Handle complaints and grievances quickly.
8. Design product as per needs of customers.

## 11. Causes of Environmental Pollution

**Definition:** Pollution is injection of harmful substances into the environment.

### 11.1 Air Pollution

1. Air Pollution lowers quality of air we breathe.
2. It has created a hole in the ozone layer.
3. Cause is smoke and chemicals from factories.
4. Cause is smoke from vehicles.

### 11.2 Water Pollution

1. Water Pollution is dumping of chemical waste in rivers and lakes.
2. Factories near rivers dump waste into water.
3. This pollutes rivers, lakes and streams.

### 11.3 Land Pollution

1. Land Pollution is dumping of toxic waste on land.
2. It affects fertility of land.
3. It makes land unfit for agriculture.
4. Task one is improve quality of damaged land.
5. Task two is check further contamination.

| Type | Meaning | Main Cause |
|------|---------|------------|
| Air Pollution | Harmful substances in air | Smoke from factories and vehicles |
| Water Pollution | Waste in rivers and lakes | Factories dump waste in water |
| Land Pollution | Toxic waste on land | Dumping reduces fertility |

CHECK: Smoke from factories causes what? Answer: Air pollution.

## 12. Need for Pollution Control

### 12.1 Ensure Safety

1. Pollution and smoke reduce visibility.
2. Poor visibility increases accidents on highways.
3. Poor visibility is hazardous for landing of aeroplanes.
4. Control improves visibility.
5. Control reduces accidents on highways and in landing and taking off of aeroplanes.

### 12.2 Economic Losses

1. Air pollution blackens buildings.
2. Water pollution affects sea life.
3. Land pollution affects fertility.
4. All these bring heavy economic losses.
5. Example is Taj Mahal loses beauty due to industries around it.

### 12.3 Maintain Natural Beauty

1. Natural beauty is mountains, rivers, trees and land.
2. Pollution affects all these.
3. Polluted rivers spread foul odour.
4. Buildings get blackened with pollution.

### 12.4 Ensure Healthy Life

1. Air pollution causes heart and lung diseases.
2. Water pollution causes liver and kidney problems.
3. Pollution control keeps a check on these diseases.

### 12.5 Lead a Comfortable Life

1. Pollution creates nuisance and inconvenience.
2. Polluted rivers become unfit for swimming and fishing.
3. Air pollution causes eye irritation, cough and breathing problems.
4. Control reduces inconvenience.

CHECK: Taj Mahal example shows what? Answer: Economic loss.

Points:

4. Participate in plantation of trees.
5. Participate in cleaning rivers and ponds.
6. Use eco marks by producing eco-friendly products.

8. Spread awareness about eco-friendly techniques and products.

## 14. What is Business Ethics

**Definition:** Business Ethics decides expected conduct of businessman and not expected conduct.

Points:

1. Ethical behaviour is above behaviour required by law.
2. Both law and ethics decide what is right and what is wrong.
3. Laws reflect ethical values of society.
4. Laws show what conduct is permitted and what is not permitted.
5. If one breaks law, one is punished.
6. Social Responsibility is broader than legal responsibility.

CHECK: Is ethics same as law? Answer: No. Ethics is above law.

## 15. Elements of Business Ethics

### 15.1 Top Management Commitment

1. Top management must guide organisation to ethical behaviour.
2. CEO must be openly committed to ethical conduct.
3. Top managers must guide middle and low level staff.

### 15.2 Publication of a Code

1. Ethical organisations define a code of conduct.
2. Code is a written document of ethics.
3. Code covers health and safety at work.
4. Code covers fair dealing in selling and marketing.

### 15.3 Establishment of Compliance Mechanism

1. Establish system to ensure decisions match ethical standards.
2. Example is ethics training in recruitment and selection.
3. Employees must be free to report unethical behaviour.

### 15.4 Involving Employees at All Levels

1. Involve employees in making ethical programmes.
2. Reason is employees must practise them.
3. Form small groups of employees.
4. Discuss ethical policies and check attitude.

### 15.5 Measuring Results

1. It is difficult to measure ethical results.
2. But verify work as per ethical standards.
3. Audit from time to time.
4. Top level must meet employees to set future plan.

| Element | Simple Meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Top Commitment | Top managers show ethical example |
| Code | Write rules in a document |
| Compliance | Check that rules are followed |
| Involve Employees | Ask staff before making rules |
| Measure Results | Audit and plan next action |

CHECK: Code is what? Answer: Written document of ethics.

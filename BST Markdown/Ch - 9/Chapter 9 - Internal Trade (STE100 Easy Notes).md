## 1. What is Internal Trade

**Definition:** Internal Trade is buying and selling within limits of a country.

Points:

1. Buying and selling takes place within a country.
2. Payments are made in home currency only.
3. Payments are received in home currency only.
4. No formalities are needed.
5. Or very few formalities are needed.
6. Local modes of transport are used.

CHECK: Say the definition of Internal Trade in one sentence.

### 1.1 Types of Internal Trade

Points:

1. Wholesale Trade sells in large quantities.
2. Retail Trade sells in small lots.

| Type | Simple Meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| Wholesale Trade | Sell in large quantities |
| Retail Trade | Sell in small lots to consumers |

CHECK: Name two types of Internal Trade. Answer: Wholesale and Retail.

## 2. What is Wholesale Trade

**Definition:** Wholesale Trade is trade in large quantities.

Points:

1. Wholesaler buys directly from manufacturer.
2. Wholesaler buys in bulk.
3. Wholesaler sells in small lots to retailer.
4. Wholesaler is link between manufacturer and retailer.
5. Wholesaler is first link in chain.

### 2.1 Features of Wholesale Trade

Points:

1. Wholesaler is link between producer and retailer.
2. Wholesaler deals in large quantities.
3. Wholesaler deals in one line only.
4. Example is stationery and foodgrains.
5. Wholesaler invests large capital in stock.
6. Wholesaler sells in small lots to retailers.
7. Wholesaler generally does not sell to ultimate consumer.
8. Generally margin of profit is low.

CHECK: Wholesaler is link between whom? Answer: Manufacturer and Retailer.

**Definition:** GST is tax on supply of goods and services.

Points:

1. GST started in India from July 2017.
2. Earlier taxes were service tax and VAT.
3. Earlier tax was excise duty also.
4. GST merged these taxes into one.
5. GST is single tax at national level.

## 23. Key Features of GST

Points:

1. GST applies in whole country.
2. GST applies on supply of goods or services.
3. GST is destination based consumption tax.
4. Earlier tax was origin based tax.
5. Import is treated as interstate supply.
6. Import pays IGST plus customs duty.
7. CGST, SGST and IGST rates are fixed together.
8. Rates are fixed by centre and states.

## 26. Solved Questions From Source

**Q1. Wholesaler is a link between?**
A: Manufacturer and Retailer. Use (c) Manufacturer and Retailer.

**Q2. What do you mean by Internal Trade?**
A: Buying and selling within limits of country is Internal Trade.

**Q3. Large quantity buying is characteristic of which trade?**
A: Wholesale Trade.

**Q4. Which is function of wholesaler?**
A: Purchasing goods from manufacturers in bulk. Use (c).

**Q5. Niranjan buys in bulk from makers and supplies to shops with advance payment. Which business? Name two parties.**
A: Wholesale Trade. Parties are Manufacturer and Retailer.

A: Itinerant retailer. Category is Periodic Market Trader.

**Q7. Birju sells vegetables on hand cart moving place to place. Which retailer?**
A: Hawkers and Pedlars. Moves street to street and supplies at doorstep.

**Q8. Aman sells low cost footwear on fixed days in markets. Classify?**
A: Itinerant retailer. Category is Periodic Market Trader.

**Q9. Harmeet Singh has large outlet with textiles, footwear, cosmetics in divisions. Identify?**
A: Departmental Store. Many departments under one roof.

**Q10. PQR Ltd sells organic products through identical outlets with uniform price and look. Identify?**
A: Chain Stores or Multiple Shops. Uniform price and uniform decoration.

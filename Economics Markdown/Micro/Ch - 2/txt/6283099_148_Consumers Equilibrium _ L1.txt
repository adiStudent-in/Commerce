¢ Do not get confused with the dictionary meaning of utility: The dictionary
meaning of utility is given as Usefut!lness/-However, to an economist, higher
utility does not mean greater usefulness. A commodity may not be useful,
yet it may have utility for a particular person. For example, liquor is
considered to be harmful for health, yet it may have a high degree of utility
for an alcoholic.

e Satisfaction Vs Pleasure: Satisfaction must not be confused with pleasure.
In fact, satisfaction need not involve actual pleasure. For example, treatment
of a patient for his broken arm is a source of great satisfaction for the
patient. However, it does not provide him any pleasure as it is merely a relief
from pain:

e Utility is Subjective: Utility varies from person to person. For example, a
thirsty person derives more utility from water in comparison to a person who
is not thirsty.

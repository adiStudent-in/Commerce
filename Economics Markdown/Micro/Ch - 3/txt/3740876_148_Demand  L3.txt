·Change in Quantity Demanded:

purchased against specific price of the commodity.
Whenever demand for the given commodity
changes due to change in its own price, then such
change in demand is known as "Change in Quantity
Demanded". For example, if demand for Pepsi
changes due to change in its own price, then such
change in demand for Pepsi is known as change in

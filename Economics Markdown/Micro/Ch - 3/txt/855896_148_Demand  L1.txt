Demand for a commodity may be either with respect to an
individual or to the entire market.
· Individual demand refers to the quantity of a commodity
that a consumer is willing and able to buy, ateach possible
price during a given period of time.
Market demand refers to the quantity of a commodity that
all consumers are willing and able to buy, at each possible
price during a given period of time.

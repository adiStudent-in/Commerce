Relationship Between TC, TFC & TVC
Total Cost (TC))=
Total Fixed Cost (TFC)+Total Variable Cost (TVC).
TFC remains constant regardless of the level of output.
TVC increases as output increases.
TC is always greater than or equal to TFC, because TvCis always added to it.
TFC is horizontal, while TVC typically slopes upward as production rises.
TC starts at the levet of TFC when Qutput is zero, and increases with(TVC
as
production grows.
Insummary:
TFC is fixed, TVC changes with output, and TC is their sum.

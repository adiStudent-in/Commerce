collected by an investigator or agency
from its source of origin for the first
time for some specific purpose.

Difference Between Primary Data and Secondary Data
Basis
Originality
Primary Data
Primary data is original because it is collected by
the investigator himself.
Secondary Data
Next
Toppers
Secondary data is not original since investigator
makes use of data collected by other agencies.
Source Primary data is collected by some agency or
person by using the method of data collection.
Secondary data is already collected and a
processed by some person or agency and is ready
for use.
Time Factor It requires longer time for data collection. Secondary data requires less time.
Cost Factor
The collection of primary data requires a
considerable amount of money & personals as
whole plan of investigation is initiated.
The collection of secondary data is cheaper as it is
taken from published or unpublished material.

Direct Personal Investigation (Personal Interview)
Under this method, the investigator collects data by having direct
contact with the informant and conducts on-the-spot enquiry.
Suitability of this Method
When detailed information has to be collected.
When area ot investigation is limited.
When nature of enquiry is confidential.
When maximum degree of accuracy is needed.
When importance is given to originality.
